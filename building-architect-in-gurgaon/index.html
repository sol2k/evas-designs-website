01 — Included What we handle

Building Architect in Gurgaon: scope that converts intent into action.

A building architect should do more than draw rooms. We connect lifestyle, plot orientation, structure, services, approvals and budget into one buildable design.

Plot & brief assessment

We study plot dimensions, orientation, road width, setbacks, family requirements, future expansion plans and the practical constraints that shape the design.

Concept planning & zoning

The first layout organises public, private and service zones so circulation, ventilation, privacy and daylight are resolved before detailing begins.

2D floor plans

Dimensioned plans for each level, including furniture logic, shafts, staircases, balconies, parking and service areas — ready for discussion and revision.

3D elevation design

Exterior massing and front elevation studies that show proportion, materials, lighting intent and the building’s presence from the street.

Approval drawing coordination

Where included in the proposal, we prepare and coordinate sanction-ready drawings and support the municipal submission process.

Structural & services coordination

We coordinate architectural intent with qualified structural, electrical, plumbing and HVAC consultants so the design is practical to execute.

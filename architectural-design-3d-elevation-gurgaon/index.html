01 — Included What we handle

Architectural Design and 3D Elevation in Gurgaon: scope that converts intent into action.

Every design package should state exactly what you receive. We keep concepts, plans, elevations, materials and revision boundaries clear so the work can move into pricing or execution without confusion.

Architectural concepts

Initial design direction based on plot, brief, orientation, privacy, ventilation, preferred style and the practical purpose of each space.

2D floor plans

Dimensioned layouts showing rooms, circulation, staircases, shafts, balconies, parking, service zones and furniture logic.

3D exterior elevation

Front and relevant side views that communicate massing, facade rhythm, materials, lighting intent and street presence.

Material & finish direction

A coordinated palette for exterior cladding, stone, paint, metal, glass and lighting so the elevation is buildable, not just attractive.

Interior design coordination

Where required, architectural openings, ceiling heights, lighting and furniture layouts are aligned with the eventual interior design.

Clear drawing handover

Final drawings are organised for client review, consultant coordination, contractor pricing or the next stage of execution.

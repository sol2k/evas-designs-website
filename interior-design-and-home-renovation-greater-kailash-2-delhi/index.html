07 — FAQ GK-2 renovation questions

Interior design & renovation in Greater Kailash 2, answered plainly.

What homeowners in Greater Kailash 2 ask us before starting.

In 2026, home renovation in Greater Kailash 2 costs roughly ₹1,400–2,200 per sq ft for a standard scope (new kitchen, bathrooms, flooring, electrical and plumbing upgrades), ₹2,200–3,500 per sq ft for a premium renovation with layout changes and designer finishes, and ₹3,500+ per sq ft for a luxury kothi renovation with facade and structural work. A typical 2,000 sq ft flat in GK-2 lands between ₹28 lakh and ₹70 lakh; a 3,000 sq ft kothi with structural and facade scope runs ₹60 lakh to ₹1.1 crore. South Delhi sits at the top of NCR pricing because of older structures and higher finish expectations.

Interior design fees in GK-2 typically run ₹50–150 per sq ft for standalone design services, or are absorbed into a turnkey contract when the same studio executes the work — which is how most of our GK-2 projects are structured. A full interior design and fit-out for a 3BHK flat (1,400–1,800 sq ft) usually costs ₹20–45 lakh including the kitchen, wardrobes, ceilings, lighting, furniture and styling. The kitchen and wardrobes are 40–50% of a typical interior budget.

A full flat renovation in GK-2 takes 8–14 weeks; a kothi renovation with structural, facade or waterproofing scope takes 4–7 months. GK-2's 30–40 year old kothis usually need 2–3 weeks of strip-out and discovery before the real scope is known. Between November and January, GRAP restrictions can pause dust-generating work like demolition and masonry, so we sequence the calendar around winter — the same schedule logic as Gurgaon, since the rules are NCR-wide.

Internal, non-structural renovation needs no MCD approval. Flats and builder floors in GK-2 need an RWA or apartment-association NOC before work starts, with fixed working hours and debris rules. In plotted kothis, internal work is free of approvals, but structural changes — adding a floor, extending covered area, or altering the facade line — need revised sanctioned plans from MCD, plus compliance with the MPD's floor-area rules for the plot. All sites must follow dust and construction-waste norms, with GRAP restrictions in winter.

Renovate when the frame is sound — a premium renovation costs ₹2,200–3,500 per sq ft against ₹3,500–5,000+ per sq ft to demolish and rebuild a kothi, plus 12–20 months and fresh MCD approvals. Rebuild when the foundation or frame is compromised, when you want a floor the old structure can't carry, or when renovation quotes cross roughly 70% of reconstruction cost — beyond that line, a rebuild gives you a new asset for marginally more money.

Livspace and HomeLane are commercial interior-furnishing companies built around factory-made modular packages — they don't do demolition, masonry, waterproofing, rewiring or structural strengthening, which is most of the work in a 30–40 year old GK-2 home. Evas Designs is a design-build studio: architecture, civil work, services and interiors by one in-house team under one contract. For a modular-kitchen-and-wardrobes refresh in a newer flat, the big brands are competitive; for a kothi renovation, they aren't in that business.

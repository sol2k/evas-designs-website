1 Written itemised scope, no surprise bills

Quick answer: A west facing house is Vastu compliant when the main entrance sits in the 3rd to 6th pada of the west wall counted from the north-west corner, heavy rooms anchor the south-west, the kitchen sits in the south-east and the pooja room in the north-east. 01 · The honest truth Myth vs architecture

The west's real issue is the evening sun — and it is a design problem with a design answer.

West facing homes fail or succeed on heat management, not on any curse. Here is what actually plays out on a west plot in Delhi NCR, season by season.

  • The complaint is specific: 3 pm to 6 pm summer sun. Low-angle western radiation is the hardest heat a building in North India receives, and it lands exactly when outdoor temperatures peak. Rooms with big west glass become unusable without management.
  • The classical answer was the verandah. Old havelis put a deep shaded verandah, jaali or chajja on the west — a buffer that cooled the rooms behind it. Every effective modern solution is the same idea in new materials.
  • Design shields beat orientation. Deep chajjas, jaali screens, pergolas, west-side service rooms and low-E or heat-reflective glass reduce the gain by 60 to 80 percent. Put the staircase, kitchen, toilets and store on the west edge and the bedrooms never meet the sun.
  • Winter changes the verdict. The same west sun is welcome warmth in December and January. Shading that is seasonal — deciduous planting, adjustable screens — gives a west facing house the best of both ends of the year.
  • In Vastu, the west belongs to Varuna. The deity of water, rain and cosmic order — associated with prosperity, fulfilment and evening rest. The texts consider west facing auspicious for traders, business owners and families whose evenings are their real family time.
02 · The entrance The rule that matters most

The pada map: where the main door goes on a west wall.

Nine padas on the west wall, counted from the north-west corner. The central stretch — Varuna's own zone — is where the main entrance earns its best reading.

West-wall padasLocationVerdict for the main entrance
1 to 2Toward the north-west corner (Vayu zone)Acceptable for shops and clinics; for homes, usable with a foyer correction
3 to 6The central stretch of the west wallBest zone: prosperity, stability and Varuna's blessing. The main entrance belongs here
7 to 9Toward the south-west cornerAvoid for the entrance; keep the south-west heavy, high and solid as the house's anchor

The door itself, per the texts

Make it the largest door in the house, of good solid wood, opening clockwise. A nameplate at eye level, an intact threshold, and a clean, obstruction-free approach. On west plots the texts also approve of a slightly elevated plinth toward the west and south, which happens to be exactly what good storm-water design wants.

A west entrance doubles as climate design

A recessed west entry with a shaded porch does Vastu duty and thermal duty at once: it puts the door in the right pada, keeps the rain off it in the monsoon, and stops the low sun from striking the foyer glass. Rules and physics pointing at the same detail is where Vastu-compliant design gets easy.

The west-side buffer strategy

The smartest west facing plans put the staircase, kitchen, toilets, store and shafts along the western edge: rooms that tolerate heat and do not need evening views. Bedrooms and the living room open east and north instead. Vastu-compliant by zone, thermally comfortable by physics — the same walls doing both jobs.

A study in the west is a gift

The texts place learning and accounts in the west and south-west, with the desk facing north or east. Evening light from a shaded west window is steady and warm — the single best natural light in the house for reading and screens. Vastu got this one right before desk lamps existed.

04 · Climate-first design Shading, glass and planting

Designing the west facade: the five moves that end the evening-heat complaint.

This is the section most Vastu pages skip and every west-facing owner needs. These five moves, in order of cost and impact.

  • Deep chajjas and fins. Horizontal projections of 2.5 to 3 feet above west windows block the high summer sun and admit the low winter sun — the cheapest climate device in architecture, and standard on our west facades.
  • Jaali screens and pergolas. A jaali outside the glass stops glare, diffuses light and still moves air; a pergola over a west balcony makes it a year-round room. Both are also the classic west-facing vernacular look.
  • Right glass. Heat-reflective or low-E glazing on the west cuts solar gain by half without darkening the room. On budgets, external shading first, glass second; both together for west bedrooms.
  • Deciduous planting. A tree or climber that leafs in summer and thins in winter tunes the facade seasonally. Amaltas, gulmohar and grape vines all do this on Delhi NCR sites.
  • Buffer rooms on the west edge. Staircase, kitchen, store and toilets absorb the heat so living and sleeping rooms never see it. This is the floor-plan version of the old haveli verandah, and it costs nothing extra.
05 · Corrections Existing homes

Already living in a west facing house? The fixes start under Rs 5,000.

Corrections follow the same hierarchy as everywhere: geometry first, elements second — and honest advice about which problems need neither.

Entrance corrections

If the main door sits on the wrong pada, a recessed foyer screen, a planter line or a corrected threshold establishes the effective entrance zone without structural work. The door swing and the nameplate are ten-minute fixes with outsized symbolic weight.

Heat corrections

External bamboo or fabric blinds, a retractable awning, thicker curtains with pelmets, and film on west glass deliver most of the shading benefit at rental-friendly cost. These also happen to be the corrections your electricity bill notices first.

Weight corrections

An empty or low south-west gets a wardrobe wall, a stone-top console or heavy art; the master suite is the premium fix where renovation allows. Weight, height and solidity — the vocabulary the south-west corner answers to in every orientation.

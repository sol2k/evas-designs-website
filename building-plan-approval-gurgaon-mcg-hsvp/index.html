Approvals · Gurgaon & Haryana

Building plan approval in Gurgaon, explained before the system wastes your season.

Every year, plot owners in Gurgaon lose a full construction season to approval confusion: they file with the wrong authority, discover a missing NOC at week six, or learn at plinth level that the sanctioned drawing and the site do not match. The process itself is not complicated once you know which of the three authorities owns your plot, what the portal actually checks, and which documents get applications rejected. This guide lays out the complete 2026 picture: MCG, HSVP and DTCP jurisdictions, the Haryana Building Code process, the document checklist, fee heads, realistic timelines, and the traps we rescue projects from. We prepare and file sanction drawings as registered architects and build what we get sanctioned, so the approval and the construction speak to each other from day one.

1 Written itemised scope, no surprise bills

Quick answer: In Gurgaon in 2026, building plans for residential plots are sanctioned by HSVP in its sectors, by the Municipal Corporation Gurugram (MCG) in municipal-limit areas and old city, and within DTCP-licensed colonies by the coloniser's process under DTCP oversight, all filed online through the Haryana ULB portal by a registered architect under the Haryana Building Code 2017 as amended. The scrutiny fee is Rs 10 per sq m of covered area; a typical 250 sq yd residential sanction takes 30 to 60 days with a complete file. After sanction you build to the approved drawings, pass plinth-level inspection, and finish with a completion and occupancy certificate.

Why jurisdiction errors cost a season

A file submitted to the wrong office is not transferred; it is returned after weeks in queue. In Gurgaon's building calendar, a six-week error in October can push your start past the winter GRAP restrictions and into the next working season.

Stilt plus four floors

Haryana permits stilt plus four floors on residential plots meeting the road-width and parking conditions, which has changed the economics of every Gurgaon plot. The additional FAR is purchasable at prevailing rates. We model this upside into the sanctioned design so you do not refile later.

Realistic timeline, residential plot

Document assembly takes one to two weeks, scrutiny and site inspection two to four weeks, and the sanction letter lands in 30 to 60 days for a clean file. Add two to six weeks for every NOC or title defect that surfaces mid-process. We state the timeline in the engagement letter, not verbally.

After sanction: the part owners skip

Build to the sanctioned drawings, call the plinth-level inspection when the plinth is cast, and close with a completion and occupancy certificate before regular use. Skipping the OC is what makes a house hard to sell, mortgage or insure later, and it converts honest owners into compounding cases.

Sanction validity

A sanction lapses if construction does not begin and progress within its validity window; extensions are available on application before expiry. We diarise validity dates for every client so a lapsed sanction never forces a fresh fee cycle.

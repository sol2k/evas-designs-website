New flat possession checklist: what to check before taking handover.

Quick answer: before accepting keys to a new flat in Delhi NCR, verify seven documents (RERA registration, completion certificate, occupancy certificate, fire NOC, payment reconciliation, no-dues certificate and carpet area statement), run a room-by-room physical inspection covering structure, waterproofing, plumbing and electricals, and submit a dated snag list before the final instalment. Under RERA you also hold a five-year structural defect liability against the builder and interest rights for any delay.

The seven documents that decide whether you should take possession.

02: Physical inspection Room-by-room snag hunt

Inspect the flat zone by zone: the full physical checklist.

Carry a laser measure, a socket tester, a phone flashlight and a marble for level checks. Better still, book an independent snagging inspection: in Delhi NCR it costs ₹3,000 to 8,000 and pays for itself the first time it catches a bathroom waterproofing failure.

  • Visit twice if you can. Once in daylight for finishes, once after a monsoon shower for leaks and drainage.
  • Photograph everything with timestamps and wide shots, so pre-existing damage cannot be blamed on your interior team later.
  • Test together, not one by one. Run all taps at once to expose pressure and drainage limits.
  • Check the flat below. Stains on its ceiling are your floor's waterproofing report.
  • Log defects before the last payment. A dated snag list is your leverage for rectification.
Zone What to check Common defect
Walls and ceiling Tap every tile and large plaster patch for hollowness, check cracks around beam lines and plumb with a level Hollow tiles, damp patches near bathrooms and window sills
Flooring Levels with a marble or spirit level, chips at edges, grout lines, skirting joints Lippage between tiles, cracked vitrified tiles under load points
Doors and windows Smooth swing and slide, alignment of frames, hardware completeness, weather sealing Warped frames, scratched glass, missing mosquito meshes
Plumbing Run every tap at full pressure together, fill and drain each sanitaryware item, check hot water lines Low pressure, slow floor-drain flow, leaks at wall joints
Electrical Trip test every MCB, socket polarity with a plug-in tester, earthing, doorbell, TV and data points Open earthing, reversed polarity, dead points
Waterproofing Flood test bathrooms and the utility balcony for 24 hours, inspect the flat below if accessible Seepage at floor-wall junctions, stained ceiling corners
Kitchen Platform level, sink drainage and overflow, provision points for appliances, gas pipe routing Blocked drain traps, no provision for RO or dishwasher
Safety Balcony and utility railing height and rigidity, emergency lighting in common areas, refuge floor access Railing below safe height, wobbly grills
03: Your legal cover Government rules that protect you

RERA and municipal rules that work for buyers at handover.

These are the regulations builders hope you have not read. Each one converts a common handover problem into an enforceable right.

Rule or regulator What it gives you Where to use it
RERA Section 14 Five-year structural defect liability from possession; builder must rectify without charge Snag list follow-ups and rectification demands
RERA Section 18 Interest for delayed possession, or full refund with interest if you exit Delayed handover claims against the promoter
RERA carpet area definition A measurable standard for area verification with refund rights on shortfall Area mismatch disputes at handover
HRERA (Gurgaon) State authority for Gurugram project complaints and compensation orders Projects in Gurugram and adjoining Haryana areas
UP RERA (Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad) State authority for NCR Uttar Pradesh project complaints Projects across Noida and Ghaziabad
Municipal completion certificate Proof the building matches the sanctioned drawings Verify with MCG, MCD or the relevant development authority before acceptance
Occupancy certificate plus fire NOC Legal right to occupy, insurable title and sanctioned utilities Refuse handover until both are issued and shown

If possession is late: write to the builder invoking Section 18 of RERA and keep proof of delivery. If keys are offered without an occupancy certificate, refuse in writing and file with HRERA or UP RERA as applicable. Groups of buyers file stronger complaints than individuals.

04: After handover From snag list to move-in

Found defects? Sequence the fix before the interiors.

The order matters: builder rectification first, interior design and any renovation next, deep clean and move-in last. Skipping the order is how buyers end up paying for the same repair twice.

01

Submit the snag list

Dated, photographed and acknowledged by the builder. Set a written rectification deadline, typically 30 to 45 days.

02

Verify rectifications

Re-flood bathrooms, re-test pressure and re-inspect every closed defect before signing any closure letter.

03

Scope interiors or renovation

This is the point to involve an interior design and build team: layouts, cost estimates and material selections all start from the rectified shell.

07: FAQ Possession questions from Delhi NCR buyers

Documents, certificates and compensation, answered directly.

The eight questions every buyer asks in the week before handover.

Seven documents matter most: the RERA registration certificate of the project, the completion certificate from the municipal corporation, the occupancy certificate, the fire NOC, your demand letter reconciled against payment receipts, a no-dues certificate, and the carpet area statement with your registered agreement. Never accept keys in exchange for only a possession letter: the occupancy certificate is what makes the unit legal to live in.

A completion certificate confirms the building was constructed according to the sanctioned plans. An occupancy certificate confirms the completed building is fit to live in, with legal water, power and drainage connections. You can have the first without the second, and many buyers have discovered this the hard way: no occupancy certificate means utilities can be disconnected and home insurance claims can be rejected.

No. Under municipal law across Delhi NCR, occupation without an occupancy certificate is illegal, and RERA treats possession offers without an occupancy certificate as deficient performance. If your builder pressures you to take keys without one, put the refusal in writing and file a complaint with the relevant RERA authority before paying the final instalment.

Under Section 18 of RERA, if the promoter fails to deliver on the committed date, you can either withdraw with a full refund plus interest, or stay invested and claim interest for every month of delay at the RERA-prescribed rate. Complaints go to HRERA for Gurgaon projects or UP RERA for Noida, Greater Noida and Ghaziabad, and the process is designed to work without a lawyer.

RERA defines carpet area as the net usable floor area of the apartment, including internal partition walls but excluding balconies, terraces, external walls and common areas. At handover, measure every room with a laser measure and compare against the agreement. RERA allows a variation of only a few percent: any shortfall must be refunded to you with interest, and any excess above the tolerance is chargable only pro-rata.

Snagging is a systematic defect hunt carried out before you accept the flat: a trained inspector taps tiles for hollowness, flood-tests bathrooms, checks electrical polarity and earthing, tests door alignment and runs the plumbing at full pressure. Independent snagging inspections in Delhi NCR cost between ₹3,000 and 8,000 for a typical 2 or 3 BHK, and the report gives you a dated defect list to hold the builder to.

Under RERA Section 14, structural defects carry a five-year liability from possession, and finishing and workmanship defects are covered by the defect liability period in your agreement, typically one to two years. Log every defect in writing with photographs within days of handover, share the snag list with the builder and keep the acknowledgement. Slow responses are grounds for a RERA complaint.

After. Finalise the occupancy certificate, snag list and rectifications first, then brief your interior designer or renovation contractor. Designers and contractors we work with across Gurgaon and Noida routinely coordinate directly with builders on rectification lists, which stops the classic blame loop where the builder says the interior team caused the defect and vice versa.

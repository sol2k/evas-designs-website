01 — The scope What "complete" covers

A complete renovation is seven projects in one.

The reason full renovations fail is sequencing — trades done out of order, or by contractors who never meet. Here's the full scope stack, in the order it must happen.

Stage What happens Typical share of budget Duration
1. Strip-out & discovery Demolition of old finishes, debris removal, walls opened, hidden damage assessed 3 – 5% 1 – 2 weeks
2. Structural & masonry Strengthening, wall moves, new openings, lintel and beam work 8 – 12% 1 – 3 weeks
3. MEP rough-in Full electrical rewiring, new panels, CPVC plumbing lines, AC and HVAC piping 10 – 15% 2 – 3 weeks
4. Waterproofing & wet areas Bathroom sunk slabs, terrace, balconies — flood-tested before tiling 4 – 6% 1 – 2 weeks
5. Flooring & tiling Vitrified, engineered wood or marble; bathroom and kitchen tiling 12 – 18% 2 – 4 weeks
6. Ceilings, kitchen & woodwork False ceilings, modular kitchen, wardrobes, doors, panelling 30 – 40% 3 – 5 weeks
7. Finishes & fit-out Painting, fixtures, lighting, furniture, decor, deep clean, handover 15 – 20% 1 – 2 weeks

The discovery rule: in a 20+ year old Gurgaon house, budget a 10–15% contingency for what strip-out reveals — corroded pipes, dead wiring, damp, termites. A renovation quote given without a physical inspection will miss these; that's why our site inspection is free and comes first.

02 — Compliances RWA · MCG · HSVP · GRAP

The compliances behind a Gurgaon renovation.

Renovation is lighter on approvals than new construction, but in Gurgaon's condominiums and plotted colonies, the rules are real — and ignoring them stops work mid-project.

Compliance Who enforces it When it applies What to plan for
RWA / society NOC Apartment & condominium associations Before any renovation starts in a flat Application + deposit; 1 – 3 weeks; fixed working hours, usually 9 am – 5 pm weekdays
Lift & common area protection Society facility management Throughout the project Lift padding, corridor protection, daily debris clearance — fines for damage
Revised sanctioned plan HSVP / MCG Structural changes in plotted houses — load-bearing walls, added floors, covered-area extension, facade setback changes Revised drawings by a licensed architect; 30 – 90 days
Colony architectural control DLF, Sushant Lok, South City and other colony bodies Any external change — facade, boundary wall, terrace additions Design approval against colony guidelines; 1 – 4 weeks, parallel with planning
Dust & waste rules HSPCB / MCG sanitation Demolition, masonry, tile cutting Debris to authorised C&D waste points only; dust screens on open work
GRAP winter restrictions CAQM November – January, when AQI crosses thresholds Dust-generating stages pause at GRAP III–IV; sequence civil work before winter

The winter factor: between November and January, GRAP rules in Delhi NCR can halt demolition and masonry for days or weeks at a stretch. We sequence renovation calendars so dusty stages finish before Diwali — or start after February.

03 — Where the money goes Wet areas & the kitchen

Bathrooms and kitchens carry half the budget.

In almost every complete renovation we price, the kitchen plus bathrooms account for 40–50% of the total — because they concentrate waterproofing, plumbing, tiling, stone and fittings into the smallest rooms.

Why wet areas cost what they cost

A bathroom renovation is a rebuild in miniature: strip to the slab, re-waterproof and flood-test, re-plumb, re-tile, then fittings. Done right it's the longest-lasting room in the house; done cheap it's the first to leak into your neighbour's ceiling.

  • Bathroom (each): ₹1.8–3 lakh standard, ₹4–7 lakh premium — waterproofing system, tile size and CP fittings drive the spread
  • Kitchen: ₹3.5–6 lakh standard, ₹8–15 lakh premium — countertop stone, hardware brand and appliance stack decide it
  • Flood-testing is non-negotiable — every wet area is water-tested for 72 hours before tiling, and photographed for your records
  • Plumbing replaced, not patched — full CPVC re-line while walls are open; mixing old pipes with new is how leaks start

07 — FAQ Complete renovation questions

Full-home renovation in Gurgaon, answered plainly.

What homeowners ask us before starting a complete renovation in Gurgaon.

A complete or turnkey home renovation covers everything from strip-out to handover: demolition of old finishes, structural repairs and strengthening, full electrical rewiring and plumbing replacement, waterproofing of bathrooms and terraces, new flooring, kitchen and bathrooms, false ceilings and lighting, woodwork and wardrobes, painting, and the final interior fit-out with furniture and decor. One team designs it, executes it and hands back a finished home — you don't coordinate separate civil, MEP and interior contractors.

In 2026, a complete home renovation in Gurgaon costs roughly ₹1,400–2,200 per sq ft for a standard scope, ₹2,200–3,500 per sq ft for premium, and ₹3,500+ per sq ft for luxury with structural changes and imported finishes. A 2,000 sq ft apartment typically lands between ₹28 lakh and ₹70 lakh depending on scope, while a 3,000 sq ft bungalow renovation with facade and structural work runs ₹60 lakh to ₹1.1 crore. Kitchen and bathrooms alone usually carry 40–50% of the budget.

Internal, non-structural renovation needs no municipal approval. In apartments, you need an RWA or society NOC before work starts, and must follow fixed working hours, lift protection and debris-disposal rules. In plotted houses, internal work is free of approvals, but structural changes — moving load-bearing walls, adding a floor, extending covered area or changing the facade setback — require revised sanctioned plans from HSVP or MCG. All renovation sites must also follow dust-control and construction-waste rules, with tighter GRAP restrictions in winter.

A full apartment renovation takes 8–14 weeks; a bungalow or villa renovation with structural, facade or waterproofing scope takes 4–7 months. The first 2–3 weeks go to strip-out and discovery — opening walls and floors to find what the old house is hiding. Between November and January, GRAP restrictions can pause dust-generating work like demolition, masonry and tile cutting, so starting civil work in September–October or February–March keeps the calendar honest.

Whole-house renovation is 15–25% cheaper than the same work done room by room over two years, because mobilisation, debris removal, scaffolding, and MEP rough-ins happen once instead of repeatedly. It also produces a coherent design — flooring levels, ceiling lines and electrical planning align across rooms. Room-by-room only makes sense when you're living in the house during the work, and even then a phased whole-house plan with one team beats a series of unrelated contractors.

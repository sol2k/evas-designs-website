01 · The entrance Eight padas, ranked

East entrance padas, from the north-east corner down.

Divide the eastern boundary into eight equal parts starting at the north-east corner. Each pada carries a name and a classical verdict. This is the table most websites flatten into east is good; it is not that simple.

How to find your pada

Measure the eastern boundary length, divide by eight, and count from the north-east corner. On a 60 foot plot each pada is 7.5 feet, so a door centred 22 to 30 feet from the north-east corner sits in Jayant. We verify this on the sanctioned site plan before any drawing starts.

If your door already sits in a weak pada

Do not panic and do not demolish. A second ceremonial entrance used daily, a threshold and nameplate correction, a shift of the door leaf within the opening, or re-angling the approach path resolves most cases. We document the fix in the design dossier so the logic survives.

Gate versus door

The compound gate and the house door are scored separately. A gate in Jayant with the door slightly off is far better than the reverse, because the gate controls the approach energy of the whole plot.

03 · Myth and physics Why east earns its reputation

The morning sun argument, tested against Delhi NCR summers.

Strip the mythology away and east facing homes still win on building science in this climate. Here is the honest physics, and the design moves that bank the advantage.

  • Gentle heat gain. Morning sun hits the eastern facade when ambient air is still cool, so the wall absorbs far less heat than the western facade does at 4 pm in May. East facing living rooms run measurably cooler in the evenings.
  • Usable morning light. East light is bright but low-glare, ideal for kitchens, pooja rooms and breakfast spaces. It supports the classical rule that morning activity belongs in the east.
  • Lower cooling load. With bedrooms in the south-west shielded from direct sun and living areas in the east, peak AC load drops noticeably compared to a west facing twin of the same plan.
  • The caveat nobody mentions. East light fades by early afternoon, so north and east rooms need layered artificial lighting for the evening. A good east facing plan budgets its lighting as carefully as its windows.

For the solar maths behind all four orientations, read east facing vs west facing buildings. For the other three directions, see our plans for north facing, south facing and west facing homes.

Corrections we design most often

  • Entrance leaf shifted within the existing opening to reach pada 3 or 4
  • A daily-use side entrance created through a utility or study in a strong pada
  • Kitchen hob turned to face east with the cook facing east, without moving the kitchen
  • Bed headboard moved to the south or west wall, sleeping with head to the south
  • Mirror and TV positions fixed to stop doubling the entrance or the bed
  • North-east corner decluttered and lightened: a water element, prayer shelf or simply open space

What we refuse to sell

  • Demolition as a first answer to a weak pada
  • Pyramids, crystals and gadgets priced as structural fixes
  • Fear-based audits where every room is declared defective
  • Vastu advice that ignores structure, plumbing and ventilation

· Process The Evas method

How a vastu-aligned home gets planned, end to end.

From orientation study to handover dossier, vastu is resolved inside the architecture, not layered on top of it.

01

Site and orientation study

We confirm true north on site, read the plot proportions, road position and surrounding masses, and map where light and heat actually fall through the day. Vastu decisions made without this step are guesswork.

02

Zoning plan

Rooms are placed zone by zone: entrance pada, kitchen, bedrooms, toilets and staircases located to satisfy both the classical matrix and modern services. You see two or three options with trade-offs stated plainly.

03

Structure and services integration

Columns, beams, shafts, tanks and panels are positioned so the vastu zoning survives structural reality. This is where architect-led vastu differs from a consultant's sketch over a builder's plan.

04

Interior alignment

Furniture layouts, bed and desk orientations, hob and sink positions, mirror and TV placement are resolved in the interior drawings, with remedies built into design rather than pasted on as objects.

05

Handover with a vastu dossier

You receive the final zoning map, the pada calculation for your entrance, and a room-by-room reference card, so every future decision, from a new study table to a renovation, stays aligned.

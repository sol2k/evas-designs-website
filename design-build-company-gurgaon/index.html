01 — The model Design-build vs the old way

What "design & build" means — and why it beats the architect-plus-contractor split.

In the traditional model you hire an architect to draw, then a contractor to build, and you referee between them for 18 months. Design-build collapses that into one contract.

Factor Design & build (one team) Architect + separate contractor
Contracts One — design, approvals, construction, interiors Two to four — architect, structural engineer, contractor, interior firm
Accountability Single point — the studio that drew it, builds it and answers for it Split — design faults and execution faults get blamed on each other
Cost certainty Itemised BOQ locked at design stage, because the builder priced the drawings Real cost emerges only after tendering — often 15–25% above the architect's estimate
Timeline Design and approvals run parallel with site prep; construction starts earlier Sequential — design, then tender, then build; each handoff loses weeks
Changes mid-project Decided in one room, priced in days Three-way negotiation between you, architect and contractor
Approvals & liaison Included — plan sanction, colony rules, inspections handled by the team Architect files the plan; everything after that is usually yours to chase

When the split model still makes sense: very large commercial builds with a professional project-management consultant, or clients who want competitive bidding on construction and are prepared to manage the process. For a family home in Gurgaon, design-build wins on cost, time and sanity. See how we run a full project on our house construction from scratch page.

02 — Government compliances HSVP · MCG · colony rules

The approvals and compliances behind every Gurgaon build.

The paperwork is half the project. Here's the full compliance stack for a plotted house in Gurgaon — what it is, who issues it, and what it costs in time.

Compliance Authority When it's needed Typical timeline
Building plan sanction HSVP (HSVP sectors) / MCG (municipal & licensed colonies) Before any construction; revised plans before structural changes 30 – 90 days
Site demarcation HSVP / MCG field office Before excavation — confirms plot boundaries and road alignment 1 – 3 weeks
Structural safety certificate Registered structural engineer Filed with the building plan; re-certified for added floors With plan filing
Labour cess & BOCW registration Haryana Labour Welfare Board Before construction starts — 1% cess on construction cost 1 – 2 weeks
Colony / RWA clearances DLF, Sushant Lok, South City and other colony bodies Architectural control approval, working hours, debris and access rules 1 – 4 weeks, parallel
Fire NOC Haryana Fire Services Buildings above 15 m height 4 – 8 weeks
Dust & environmental compliance HSPCB / CAQM (GRAP) Throughout construction — dust screens, C&D waste rules, winter restrictions Ongoing
Completion + occupation certificate HSVP / MCG After construction — mandatory before lawful occupancy 1 – 3 months

Where projects stall: plan rejection for FAR, height or setback violations is the #1 schedule killer — each revision resets the 30–90 day clock. And between November and January, GRAP winter restrictions in Delhi NCR can pause dust-generating stages entirely. A design-build team that files clean drawings and sequences the calendar around GRAP saves you a full season.

What in-house design looks like

Every plan is drawn by architects who know exactly what our construction teams can execute, at what cost. There is no beautiful-but-unbuildable drawing, no detail that dies on site because the contractor "doesn't do it that way."

07 — FAQ Design-build questions

Building in Gurgaon, answered plainly.

What plot owners ask us before starting a turnkey project in Gurgaon.

A design and build company takes a project from a blank plot to a finished, furnished home under one contract: architectural design and 3D elevations, structural engineering, government approvals and plan sanction, construction, and interior fit-out. Instead of you coordinating an architect, a structural engineer, a contractor and an interior designer separately, one team owns the entire result — and the accountability that comes with it.

In 2026, turnkey design-build construction in Gurgaon runs roughly ₹2,200–2,800 per sq ft for a standard specification, ₹2,800–3,800 per sq ft for premium, and ₹3,800–5,500+ per sq ft for luxury builds with imported finishes and automation. A 300 sq yard plot built as stilt plus four floors (about 8,000–9,000 sq ft) typically lands between ₹1.8 crore and ₹3.2 crore all-in. Design fees are usually absorbed into the contract when the same studio builds what it designs.

For a plotted house in Gurgaon you need: a sanctioned building plan from HSVP (for HSVP sectors) or MCG (for municipal areas and licensed colonies), site demarcation, a structural safety certificate from a registered structural engineer, labour cess registration under BOCW, and after construction a completion certificate followed by an occupation certificate. Buildings above 15 metres also need a fire NOC. Gated colonies like DLF and Sushant Lok additionally enforce their own architectural control and construction-hour rules.

A residential building plan in Gurgaon typically takes 30–90 days to sanction through the online single-window portal, depending on plot category and how clean the drawings are — plans that violate FAR, height or setback norms get bounced back for revision, which resets the clock. Sanctioned plans are valid for two years, extendable on request. An experienced design-build team files drawings that pass scrutiny the first time, which is where most of the schedule is won or lost.

Plan sanction adds 1–3 months upfront.

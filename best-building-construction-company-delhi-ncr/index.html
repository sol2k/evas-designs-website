How to read this list

Why local studios, not the big developer names.

When you buy an apartment in a tower, the developer's scale is the product. When you build your own home on your own plot, scale works against you: you become a small file in a large system. Local design-build studios exist for the second situation. The owner can sit across a table from the architect, walk the site weekly, and call one number when something needs deciding. Every studio on this list works that way, which is why the usual big names are deliberately absent.

02 · Method Before you sign anything

What construction actually costs in Delhi NCR right now.

Per-square-foot numbers float around every construction conversation. These are honest working ranges for 2026, so you can sanity-check any quote you receive, including ours.

01

Grey structure · Rs 1,600 to 2,200 per sq ft

Foundation, columns, slabs, brickwork, plaster and basic waterproofing. The skeleton of the house, before any visible finish goes on.

02

Standard finish · Rs 2,200 to 3,000 per sq ft

Grey structure plus mid-range flooring, sanitaryware, doors, paint and electricals. The most common band for family homes in NCR colonies and sectors.

03

Premium finish · Rs 3,000 to 4,500 per sq ft

Branded fittings, engineered stone, custom joinery, designed lighting and climate-considered envelopes. The band most of our residential work sits in.

04

Fully bespoke · Rs 4,500 per sq ft and above

Architect-designed homes with imported materials, smart systems and handcrafted detail. Plot location, structural complexity and material choices all move the number.

These are indicative ranges for budgeting conversations, not quotes. Labour rates in NCR shift with season and demand, and every plot has surprises. For a real number, the only honest route is a site visit and an itemised estimate, which is exactly how we prepare ours.

03 · Method Use this on any builder

Four checks that separate a good builder from a good brochure.

Apply these to every company on this list, including Evas Designs. A builder who answers all four confidently will rarely disappoint you.

01

Who holds the accountability?

Ask one question: when something goes wrong on site, whose problem is it? With a design-build team it is one firm's. With separate architect, contractor and vendors, it becomes yours.

02

Visit a finished project, not a showroom

A completed two-year-old project tells you more than any sales gallery. Look at how joints have settled, how waterproofing has held, how the finish is ageing.

03

Demand an itemised estimate

A single lump-sum figure hides where money will quietly leak. Line-item quotes let you compare builders honestly and change scope without renegotiating everything.

04

Check the weekly reporting rhythm

Ask to see a real weekly site report from a current project. Builders who document progress, photographs and deviations as routine behave very differently from those who improvise.

06 — How delivery runs From first drawing to keys

The same disciplined path, every project.

The process behind the portfolio above, whether the job is a 1,900 sq ft apartment or an 18,000 sq ft office.

01

Survey and brief

Site measurements, services audit and a written brief covering how you live or work, and what the budget must buy.

02

Design and materials

Plans, 3D views and a locked material board, with approvals from your estate or authority started in parallel.

03

Execution

Civil, electrical, HVAC, plumbing, joinery and finishes by our own teams, with weekly photo reports and a signed stage plan.

04

Quality and snag

Stage-wise checks against the drawings, a joint snag walk with you, and every item closed before handover day.

05

Deep clean and handover

Post-handover deep cleaning: dust extraction, marble crystallisation, glass and grout detailing, then keys, warranties and the maintenance file.

What Palam Vihar, Gurgaon clients ask us before starting.

In 2026, a refresh renovation in Palam Vihar runs ₹1,400–2,000 per sq ft, a full renovation with layout changes and MEP replacement runs ₹2,000–3,000, and villas with structural scope run ₹2,800–3,500+. A typical 2,500 sq ft Palam Vihar home lands between ₹35 lakh and ₹75 lakh depending on how much of the hidden stack gets replaced.

Less than people fear, if it is phased correctly. We work one zone at a time — bathrooms and kitchen early, bedrooms last — with dust barriers, a daily clean-down and fixed working hours. Most Palam Vihar families stay through the project; a full move-out is only needed for gut renovations.

Internal non-structural work needs no approval, but Palam Vihar colonies usually require an RWA NOC with fixed hours and debris rules. Structural changes — wall removals, floor additions, facade alterations — need revised sanction drawings from the authority. We handle both as part of the scope.

Renovate when the frame is sound: a premium renovation costs ₹2,000–3,000 per sq ft against ₹3,500–5,000+ to demolish and rebuild, plus 12–20 months and fresh sanctions. Rebuild when the structure is compromised or you need a floor the frame cannot carry — as a rule of thumb, when renovation quotes cross 70% of rebuild cost, rebuild.

A flat or builder-floor renovation takes 8–14 weeks; a villa with structural and facade scope takes 4–7 months. Discovery after strip-out can add scope, which is why our BOQ carries an open-book variation clause rather than surprise lump sums.

It is the stage we are most obsessive about. Every wet area is flood-tested for 72 hours before tiling, photographed for records, and covered by a 10-year waterproofing warranty. Leak callbacks are the single biggest dispute in Gurgaon renovations — our process exists so there are none.

Yes — single-room renovations are a third of our work. A bathroom strip-out to designer refit runs ₹2.5–8 lakh; a kitchen ₹3.5–15 lakh. Even single rooms get the full sequence: waterproofing, MEP, finishes.

We stop, document with photos and a structural engineer’s assessment, and give you a priced option sheet before touching anything further. No scope grows without a signature — that is the open-book clause in every contract.

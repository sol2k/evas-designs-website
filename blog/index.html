Questions readers ask first.

Quick answers before you dive into a guide.

For a standard finishes home in Gurgaon, most turnkey builds fall between Rs 2,200 and Rs 3,200 per square foot of built-up area in 2026, before interior furniture. Premium finishes and basements push the band higher. Our free cost calculator gives locality-wise numbers, and the house renovation cost guide explains what moves the number up or down.

The vastu and design section of this blog covers north-facing house plans with entrance padas, east-facing vs west-facing trade-offs, and griha pravesh muhurat dates for 2026. Each guide lists the common mistakes to avoid before the drawings are finalised.

Partly. GRAP stage restrictions and Delhi's winter action plan stop dust-generating outdoor work for stretches between November and January, while most indoor interior work continues. The winter construction guide and the interior-work-during-ban guide explain exactly what is allowed at each stage.

Compare on four things: cost per square foot with a written scope, who holds the contracts and accountability, the payment milestone structure, and references from finished local projects. The interior company comparison guide in this blog runs these checks across three popular options in Gurgaon.

Cost, rule and muhurat guides are reviewed at least twice a year and whenever a major policy changes, such as the Haryana Land Pooling Policy 2026 or the winter GRAP directions. Each guide shows its review date at the top of the page.

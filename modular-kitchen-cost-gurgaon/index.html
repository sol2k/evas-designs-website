02 · Cost factors Why quotes spread

The six factors that decide where in the band you land.

Two projects with the same floor plan can quote Rs 5 lakh apart for honest reasons. These are the six, in the order they actually move money.

  • Layout and size. The counter run in linear feet decides the carcass cost more than any finish does. An island adds plumbing, electrical and structure work beyond its own joinery.
  • Shutter material. Laminate, acrylic, PU paint, membrane and veneer sit in that order of price. A PU-finish kitchen costs 40 to 60 percent more than the same kitchen in laminate.
  • Hardware and channels. Soft-close drawers, tandem boxes and lift-ups from branded channels (Hettich, Hafele, Blum) add Rs 40,000 to 1.5 lakh but survive ten years of daily use.
  • Appliances. Chimney, hob, oven, microwave, dishwasher and refrigerator integration can equal the joinery cost. We quote appliances separately so you can compare fairly.
  • Services relocation. Moving the sink, hob or wet wall means plumbing and electrical rework; on upper floors of Gurgaon floors and apartments it also means waterproofing discipline.
  • Who measures and installs. A kitchen designed on site measurements fits; one ordered from a catalogue rarely does. Our survey measures wall by wall before anything is manufactured.

Included in our scope, always

  • Design and 3D visualisation before manufacture
  • Carcass, shutters, hardware and counter
  • Sink, faucet and plumbing rework
  • Electrical points, lighting and switch plates
  • Chimney, hob and appliance installation
  • Deep clean and handover with care manual

Quoted separately, upfront

  • Appliances (quoted separately at MRP or better)
  • Civil changes to wet walls unless in scope
  • Waterproofing of the kitchen slab where structural
  • GST at 18 percent on the final invoice

Structure to handover runs 14 to 18 months for a premium stilt-plus-four, weather and ban-season adjusted. Your agreement carries a written schedule with milestone payments, not vague monthly updates.

How we keep rates honest

Materials are locked at contract signing, labour is on our own rolls, and every material bill is shown at invoice. The four most inflated line items in Gurgaon construction, steel, cement, marble and electrical, are exactly the ones we open to you.

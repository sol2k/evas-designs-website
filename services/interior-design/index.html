This service is for anyone furnishing or refreshing a space in Gurgaon or Delhi NCR — a new apartment, an independent home, a villa, or an office — and wants a designed, coordinated interior rather than a room-by-room patchwork.

01 — Included What we handle

Interior design that goes past the mood board.

Interior design should resolve how a space works, not just how it photographs. We connect layout, light, materials and furniture into one coordinated plan.

Space & lifestyle assessment

We study the floor plan, how the household or team actually uses the space, storage needs and daily movement before a layout is drawn.

Concept & mood boards

A design direction — palette, materials, lighting intent and furniture language — agreed with you before detailing begins.

3D visualisation

Photorealistic 3D views of every space, so you can see and approve the design before a single order is placed.

Material & finish selection

Flooring, wall finishes, stone, veneers, hardware and lighting curated to the concept and your budget.

Custom furniture & styling

Furniture designed and built to fit the space exactly, plus soft furnishing and styling for the final reveal.

Execution coordination

Our own site team manages vendors, quality checks and schedule — so design intent survives all the way to handover.

How we define the work

Clear scope before a single order is placed.

Interior projects go wrong when design, procurement and execution are handled by three different people with three different incentives. Our proposals lay out design deliverables, procurement and on-site coordination clearly, under one team.

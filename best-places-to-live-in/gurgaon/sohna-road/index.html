Living on Sohna Road: where Gurgaon actually lives.

Sohna Road is Gurgaon's lived-in middle: established societies, functioning markets, some of the city's best value per sq ft, and a straight run to KMP Expressway and the industrial belt. Rates run Rs 10,000 to 15,000 per sq ft, which means a family 3BHK costs what a studio costs on Golf Course Road. Here is what each budget buys, society by society.

The renovation lever

Tired stock in every one of these bands trades at a 20 to 35 percent discount to rebuilt or new stock, and a full renovation costs 15 to 20 percent of the new price. Buy tired, renovate well, hold long: it is the most repeatable arbitrage in Delhi NCR housing.

Interiors are the second budget

Whatever you buy, the home you live in is the purchase plus the interior. Budget Rs 2,150 to 3,000 per sq ft for a full premium interior or Rs 950 to 1,400 for a selective upgrade, and review the floor plan before you sign, not after.

07 Asked on every Sohna Road visit

Your questions, answered plainly.

Entry options start near Rs 1.2 to 1.8 crore for two bedroom units in older societies such as Greenwoods and Rosewood City. The family heartland of Rs 1.8 to 3 crore covers three bedroom homes in Malibu Towne, Vipul Greens and Orchid Petals. Rs 3 to 4.5 crore reaches Malibu Towne duplexes, larger four bedroom formats and independent floors near Sectors 47 and 49, and premium floors and villas start beyond that.

Malibu Towne remains the default family answer: large green campus, functioning club, established resident community and school buses from most major schools. Vipul Greens and Orchid Petals suit buyers who want similar space at slightly lower outlay, while Sispal Vihar's plotted floors appeal to those who want land ownership with city convenience.

To Gurgaon's office belt, yes: Cyber City and Udyog Vihar run twenty to thirty minutes north via the Golf Course Road connector or the expressway. To Delhi it is weaker than the Golf Course corridors, with the KMP Expressway and NH-48 doing most of the work. Most residents treat it as Gurgaon-internal living with good access south to Manesar and KMP.

Sohna Road's stock is fifteen to twenty years old, so this is renovation country. A selective upgrade, meaning kitchen, two bathrooms, wardrobes and ceilings, runs Rs 950 to 1,400 per sq ft and 8 to 12 weeks. A full luxury makeover runs Rs 2,150 to 3,000 per sq ft. Our Sohna Road interior design guide prices both paths.

Malibu Towne and Vipul Greens score well: internal roads you can actually walk, parks, markets at the gate and doctors within ten minutes. The one detail to check is lift access in the tower you shortlist, as a few older blocks have reliability issues.

If you need to live in the home within a year, Sohna Road wins outright: you see the actual flat, the neighbours and the maintenance record before paying. New Gurgaon along Dwarka Expressway offers newer stock at similar or lower rates but with longer possession horizons. The two compete directly, and the honest answer depends on your timeline, not the brochure.

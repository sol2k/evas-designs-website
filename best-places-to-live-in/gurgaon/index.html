The best places to live in Gurgaon, judged street by street.

Gurgaon is not one housing market, it is five. A Rs 2 crore budget buys a premium 3BHK on Sohna Road, a compact flat near DLF City, and nothing at all on Golf Course Road. Premium buyers: two roads

Golf Course Road buys you the finished city; Golf Course Extension Road buys you its future at roughly two-thirds the rate. The premium decision is convenience today versus space tomorrow.

Family value: two answers

DLF City for the walk-to-school, low-rise life; Sohna Road for the same square footage at lower prices and an easier upgrade cycle. Both are renovation-rich markets where tired stock rewards buyers.

Growth bets: one corridor

Dwarka Expressway is the widest spread in Gurgaon: Rs 1.2 crore value homes to Rs 10 crore villas on one road. Its interior guide prices every project on the corridor.

The renovation lever

Tired stock in every one of these bands trades at a 20 to 35 percent discount to rebuilt or new stock, and a full renovation costs 15 to 20 percent of the new price. Buy tired, renovate well, hold long: it is the most repeatable arbitrage in Delhi NCR housing.

Interiors are the second budget

Whatever you buy, the home you live in is the purchase plus the interior. Budget Rs 2,150 to 3,000 per sq ft for a full premium interior or Rs 950 to 1,400 for a selective upgrade, and review the floor plan before you sign, not after.

07 Asked by every family we meet

Your questions, answered plainly.

For most families the answer is DLF City (Phases 1 to 5) or Golf Course Extension Road. DLF City wins on schools, walkability and low-rise calm; Golf Course Extension Road wins on newer stock, larger floor plans and better value per sq ft than Golf Course Road. Budget families usually land on Sohna Road or New Gurgaon along Dwarka Expressway, where a 3BHK is available at roughly half the Golf Course Road price.

As a working rule: Rs 1.5 to 2.5 crore on Sohna Road or New Gurgaon, Rs 2.5 to 4 crore on Golf Course Extension Road or DLF City, and Rs 4 to 7 crore on Golf Course Road in an established tower. These are starting bands, not ceilings, and older societies inside DLF City regularly surprise buyers on both sides of the range.

DLF City Phases 1 to 3 and the Golf Course Road belt sit closest to the Rapid Metro network, with Sector 42-43 and Phase 2, 3 stations serving the area. Golf Course Extension Road is catching up as the metro extends along the corridor, and Dwarka Expressway feeds the Dwarka Sector 21 interchange on the Delhi Metro Airport Express line.

Golf Course Road wins on maturity: schools, hospitals, tree cover and established neighbourhoods. Golf Course Extension Road wins on value: newer towers, larger homes and prices roughly 25 to 40 percent lower per sq ft for comparable specifications. Most families who can afford either choose Golf Course Road for convenience or Golf Course Extension Road for space; our buy-versus-build analysis covers the decision logic in detail.

DLF City Phases 1 to 3 score highest on walkability, hospital proximity and quiet streets, with Golf Course Road a close second. Within newer stock, low-density projects on Golf Course Extension Road and Ashiana-style senior-friendly projects near Sector 80 are designed with this resident profile in mind.

Ready-to-move avoids construction risk and lets you inspect the actual flat, neighbour mix and maintenance quality. Under-construction on Golf Course Extension Road or Dwarka Expressway typically prices 15 to 25 percent lower for the same finished specification. Either way, review the floor plan with a designer before you sign; our sister venture, Evas Properties, handles shortlisting across all five areas.

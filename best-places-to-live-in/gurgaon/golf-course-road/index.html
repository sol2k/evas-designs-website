Living on Golf Course Road: Gurgaon's original gold standard.

Golf Course Road is where Gurgaon's luxury story started, and it still sets the benchmark: tree-lined avenues, the Rapid Metro underneath, top schools within a ten-minute drive, and the Aravalli ridge as a backdrop. It is also the city's priciest stretch per sq ft. Here is what each budget actually buys here, society by society, and what it costs to live well once you move in.

The renovation lever

Tired stock in every one of these bands trades at a 20 to 35 percent discount to rebuilt or new stock, and a full renovation costs 15 to 20 percent of the new price. Buy tired, renovate well, hold long: it is the most repeatable arbitrage in Delhi NCR housing.

Interiors are the second budget

Whatever you buy, the home you live in is the purchase plus the interior. Budget Rs 2,150 to 3,000 per sq ft for a full premium interior or Rs 950 to 1,400 for a selective upgrade, and review the floor plan before you sign, not after.

07 Asked on every Golf Course Road visit

Your questions, answered plainly.

Entry tickets start around Rs 2.5 to 3.5 crore for compact two and three bedroom units in older towers such as Suncity and Essel Tower. Established three bedroom homes in Heritage City or The Icon run Rs 3.5 to 5.5 crore, newer DLF towers such as The Crest sit at Rs 5.5 to 8 crore, and the landmark residences, The Aralias, The Magnolias and The Camellias, range from Rs 8 crore to well above Rs 25 crore for the largest formats.

Suncity and Heritage City remain the family workhorses: large apartments, established communities, easy access to schools on the road and club facilities that actually function. For newer stock, DLF The Crest offers contemporary floor plans and full-society amenities. The choice usually comes down to age versus specification: older societies give space and community, newer towers give finishes and efficiency.

You pay a 25 to 40 percent premium on Golf Course Road for maturity: schools, hospitals, tree cover and metro access are all in place today. Golf Course Extension Road offers newer, larger homes for the same money twenty minutes south. If daily convenience matters more than square footage, the road wins; if space and new stock matter more, the extension wins. Our corridor comparison works through the numbers.

For a premium family specification budget Rs 2,150 to 3,000 per sq ft of carpet area, which lands at Rs 45 to 65 lakh for a typical 3BHK and Rs 65 lakh to 1.1 crore for a 4BHK.

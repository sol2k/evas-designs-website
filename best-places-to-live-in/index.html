Choose Gurgaon if

Your commute matters more than the address plate, you want a larger newer home per rupee, and you are comfortable with society living over neighbourhood living. Below Rs 4 crore, Gurgaon almost always wins on square footage.

Choose Delhi if

Schools, hospitals and metro coverage within a ten-minute radius matter most, you value land value security, and you prefer an established colony to a construction zone. Above Rs 4 crore, Delhi's best colonies hold value like nowhere else in NCR.

The NCR is one market now

Dwarka Expressway, Aerocity and the expressway belt have blurred the border: you can live in Gurgaon and work in Delhi, or vice versa. Our sister venture, Evas Properties, shortlists across both cities.

The renovation lever

Tired stock in DLF City, Malviya Nagar, Greater Kailash and Rohini trades at a 20 to 35 percent discount to new or rebuilt stock, and a full renovation costs 15 to 20 percent of the new price. Buy tired, renovate well, hold long: the most repeatable arbitrage in NCR housing.

Interiors are the second budget

Whatever you buy, the home you live in is the purchase plus the interior. Budget Rs 2,150 to 3,000 per sq ft for a full premium interior or Rs 950 to 1,400 for a selective upgrade, and review the floor plan before you sign, not after.

07 Asked by every family we meet

Your questions, answered plainly.

It depends on what you are optimising for. Gurgaon wins on newer stock, larger floor plans, gated services and shorter commutes to the corporate belt on Golf Course Road and the expressways. Delhi wins on established social infrastructure, school depth, land value security and metro coverage. At the same budget, Gurgaon buys you more square footage; Delhi buys you a more finished neighbourhood. Most families we work with choose Gurgaon below Rs 4 crore and Delhi above it.

In 2026, a working rule across both cities: Rs 1.5 to 2.5 crore buys a solid 3BHK on Sohna Road, New Gurgaon, Dwarka or Rohini; Rs 2.5 to 4 crore buys Golf Course Extension Road, DLF City or Malviya Nagar; Rs 4 to 7 crore buys Golf Course Road or Greater Kailash floors; and Rs 7 crore and above opens Golf Course Road landmarks, GK bungalows and DLF Phase 1 to 2 plots. Every area guide on this page prices its societies band by band.

Gurgaon: DLF City Phases 1 to 3 and Golf Course Road score highest on walkability, school proximity and quiet streets. Delhi: Greater Kailash, Vasant Kunj and Malviya Nagar combine low crime with schools, parks and hospitals inside a ten-minute radius. Dwarka and Rohini are Delhi's best-planned family belts, with sectors that function as self-contained villages.

Both work, and the maths decides. Tired stock in DLF City, Malviya Nagar, Greater Kailash and Rohini trades at a 20 to 35 percent discount to new or rebuilt stock, and a full renovation costs 15 to 20 percent of the new price. New stock on Golf Course Extension Road and Dwarka Expressway wins on possession-ready convenience. Our renovation cost guide prices both routes room by room.

Delhi: Dwarka sits on the Blue Line spine, Rohini on the Red Line, and Malviya Nagar, Greater Kailash and Vasant Kunj on the Yellow and Magenta lines. Gurgaon: DLF City Phases 1 to 3 and the Golf Course Road belt are served by the Rapid Metro, while Dwarka Expressway feeds the Dwarka Sector 21 interchange on the Airport Express line.

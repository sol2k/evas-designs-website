The renovation lever

Tired stock in every one of these bands trades at a 20 to 35 percent discount to rebuilt or new stock, and a full renovation costs 15 to 20 percent of the new price. Buy tired, renovate well, hold long: it is the most repeatable arbitrage in Delhi NCR housing.

Interiors are the second budget

Whatever you buy, the home you live in is the purchase plus the interior. Budget Rs 2,150 to 3,000 per sq ft for a full premium interior or Rs 950 to 1,400 for a selective upgrade, and review the floor plan before you sign, not after.

07 Asked on every Dwarka visit

Your questions, answered plainly.

DDA flats in sectors 1 to 12 start around Rs 1.2 to 1.8 crore for two bedroom units and Rs 1.8 to 2.5 crore for three bedroom flats in livable condition. Cooperative group housing societies run Rs 2 to 3 crore for premium three bedroom formats, and renovated or larger corner units cross Rs 3.5 crore in the better sectors.

Sectors 4 to 7 and 18 to 23 lead on livability: settled communities, working markets, the shortest walks to metro and the Biodiversity-adjacent green cover near Sector 19. Sectors 1 to 3 trade some polish for lower prices and bigger units. The airport-side sectors suit frequent flyers who do not mind aircraft noise.

On function, yes: schools including DPS Dwarka and Venkateshwar are inside the sub-city, sector markets cover daily needs, and the metro gives teenagers real independence. The trade-off is aesthetic: uniform DDA architecture and aging common areas, which is exactly what a renovation budget fixes.

DDA flats from the 1990s and 2000s are renovation-friendly shells: Rs 1,100 to 1,700 per sq ft comprehensively renews one, typically Rs 22 to 40 lakh for a 3BHK, with kitchen-led scopes starting lower. Cooperative society flats add society NOC steps that we handle as part of the paperwork scope.

Among Delhi's best on both counts. The Blue Line's nine-plus stations, the Airport Express interchange at Dwarka Sector 21, and the expressway to Gurgaon make it the best-connected sub-city in the capital. Resident societies with active RWAs and lit sector roads keep safety ratings high by Delhi standards.

Dwarka wins on metro density, airport and Gurgaon access, and overall finish level. Rohini wins on price, North West Delhi schools and Metro Red Line access. If your life is South or West Delhi plus Gurgaon, Dwarka; if it is North or Central Delhi, Rohini. Our Rohini guide prices the other side.

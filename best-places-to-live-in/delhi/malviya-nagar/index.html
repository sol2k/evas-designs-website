The renovation lever

Tired stock in every one of these bands trades at a 20 to 35 percent discount to rebuilt or new stock, and a full renovation costs 15 to 20 percent of the new price. Buy tired, renovate well, hold long: it is the most repeatable arbitrage in Delhi NCR housing.

Interiors are the second budget

Whatever you buy, the home you live in is the purchase plus the interior. Budget Rs 2,150 to 3,000 per sq ft for a full premium interior or Rs 950 to 1,400 for a selective upgrade, and review the floor plan before you sign, not after.

07 Asked on every Malviya Nagar visit

Your questions, answered plainly.

Older floors in the Khirki and Sheikh Sarai side pockets start near Rs 2 to 3.5 crore. Rebuilt three bedroom floors in Shivalik, Panchsheel Vihar and the main Malviya Nagar blocks run Rs 3.5 to 5.5 crore. Newly rebuilt floors with lift, stilt and premium finishes command Rs 5.5 to 9 crore, with corner and wide-road plots beyond that.

Shivalik and Panchsheel Vihar lead on street quality, plot sizes and neighbour profile. The blocks behind the Malviya Nagar market win on daily convenience. Khirki Extension and the southern pockets offer the lowest entry prices, with trade-offs in street width and parking. Our studio sits in Shivalik Colony, which may be why we argue for it.

Better than its reputation: the Yellow Line's Malviya Nagar station anchors the colony, Hauz Khas and its interchange are one stop north, and the airport is twenty minutes via the BRT corridor. Schools including Gyan Bharati and Apeejay are within the fifteen-minute circle, and Max Saket covers emergencies.

A comprehensive renovation of a 180 to 250 sq yd floor runs Rs 1,500 to 2,300 per sq ft of built area, typically Rs 45 lakh to 90 lakh. Add Rs 3 to 6 lakh where the staircase must be reworked for a lift. Tired-stock floors bought at Rs 2.5 to 3.5 crore routinely outperform the market after a proper renovation.

Yes, with the same lift caveat as everywhere in South Delhi. The colony's flat terrain, markets at walking distance and the metro make daily life genuinely car-optional. Rebuilt floors with lifts convert the format into proper senior living, and there is a reason so many families settle parents here.

The arithmetic usually favours renovating: tired floors trade at a 25 to 35 percent discount to rebuilt stock, while a full renovation costs 15 to 20 percent of the rebuilt price.

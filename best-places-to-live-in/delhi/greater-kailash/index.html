Living in Greater Kailash: South Delhi's benchmark.

Greater Kailash is the address Delhi measures itself against: M-Block and N-Block markets, parks inside every block, top schools at walking distance and a builder-floor culture that turns every plot into a design decision. Rates run Rs 25,000 to 45,000 per sq ft, and the gap between a tired 1980s floor and a 2025 rebuild is the whole game. Here is what each budget actually buys.

The renovation lever

Tired stock in every one of these bands trades at a 20 to 35 percent discount to rebuilt or new stock, and a full renovation costs 15 to 20 percent of the new price. Buy tired, renovate well, hold long: it is the most repeatable arbitrage in Delhi NCR housing.

Interiors are the second budget

Whatever you buy, the home you live in is the purchase plus the interior. Budget Rs 2,150 to 3,000 per sq ft for a full premium interior or Rs 950 to 1,400 for a selective upgrade, and review the floor plan before you sign, not after.

07 Asked on every GK visit

Your questions, answered plainly.

Older two and three bedroom floors in GK-1's inner blocks start around Rs 3 to 5 crore. Rebuilt floors with lift and stilt parking run Rs 5 to 8 crore in GK-1 and Rs 6 to 10 crore in GK-2 depending on block. The premium belt, GK-2 N-Block and the NRI enclave, commands Rs 10 to 15 crore-plus for designer builds, corner plots and wide-road addresses.

GK-1 offers more stock at lower prices, bigger plots in parts, and the M-Block market as its social engine. GK-2 offers wider roads, more uniform quality and the premium W and N blocks, at a 20 to 30 percent premium. Families wanting value and buzz lean GK-1; buyers wanting quiet prestige lean GK-2.

Four things decide value here: the staircase core, because it decides whether a lift can ever be added; stilt use, because covered parking is non-negotiable; the plot's position on sun and road width; and the quality of the last rebuild, because refitting someone else's compromise costs more than starting clean. Our GK-2 renovation guide walks the checklist.

A comprehensive renovation of a 200 to 250 sq yd floor runs Rs 1,600 to 2,400 per sq ft of built area, typically Rs 55 lakh to 1.1 crore depending on specification, plus Rs 3 to 6 lakh where staircase and lift cores need rework. The premium pays back: renovated floors in GK-1 command 15 to 25 percent over tired stock.

Among the best in Delhi: flat blocks, parks on every street, markets within walking distance, and emergency care at Max Saket and Apollo within twenty minutes. The single detail that decides comfort is the lift. A rebuilt floor with a proper lift converts a three-storey home into single-level living; a tired floor without one does not.

Yes, and it is the classic South Delhi move: buy tired, rebuild or comprehensively renovate, and hold an asset that behaves like new construction. Structural assessment comes first, then MCD sanction drawings, then the build. We run that sequence end to end, which is the only version of it that stays on schedule.

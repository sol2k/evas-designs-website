Living in Vasant Kunj: planned South Delhi, warts and all.

Vasant Kunj is Delhi's great planned experiment: sector blocks, wide roads, the Aravalli Biodiversity Park as a backyard, and malls that finally gave South West Delhi a centre. It is also two markets in one: DDA-built pockets where Rs 2.5 crore still buys a 3BHK, and private sectors where rebuilt floors cross Rs 8 crore. Here is what each budget buys, pocket by pocket.

The renovation lever

Tired stock in every one of these bands trades at a 20 to 35 percent discount to rebuilt or new stock, and a full renovation costs 15 to 20 percent of the new price. Buy tired, renovate well, hold long: it is the most repeatable arbitrage in Delhi NCR housing.

Interiors are the second budget

Whatever you buy, the home you live in is the purchase plus the interior. Budget Rs 2,150 to 3,000 per sq ft for a full premium interior or Rs 950 to 1,400 for a selective upgrade, and review the floor plan before you sign, not after.

The DDA-plus-renovation case

A Rs 2.8 crore DDA flat plus a Rs 45 lakh renovation routinely out-performs a Rs 5 crore private floor on both comfort and value. It is the strongest arithmetic in South West Delhi.

Farmhouse diligence

The Mahipalpur-edge farm belt gives South Delhi land at Chattarpur-discount rates, with airport proximity as a bonus. Land use, boundaries and access must be verified before money moves; walk it with a lawyer and a designer.

07 Asked on every Vasant Kunj visit

Your questions, answered plainly.

DDA pockets A and B offer entry points at Rs 2.5 to 4 crore for two and three bedroom flats. Private sectors C to F run Rs 4 to 7 crore for standard floors and Rs 7 to 12 crore for rebuilt designer floors with lift and stilt. The farmhouse belt along the Mahipalpur edge starts where the floor market ends, with usable farm plots from Rs 12 to 15 crore.

Sectors C, D and E are the balance point: private construction, active markets and the shortest walks to the malls. Sector F suits buyers wanting newer floors and quieter streets. DDA pockets A and B win purely on arithmetic, trading finishes and flexibility for location at half the private-sector rate.

Strongly. The Biodiversity Park functions as a 600-acre playground, DPS Vasant Kunj and GD Goenka sit inside the area, the malls cover everything retail, and sector schools keep commutes short. For airport-heavy families, the twenty-minute drive to T3 is a quality-of-life feature no other South Delhi address matches.

DDA flats from the 1980s and 1990s take full renewals well: Rs 1,400 to 2,100 per sq ft for a comprehensive renovation, with kitchen and bathroom re-planning the most requested scope. Private floors run Rs 1,600 to 2,400 per sq ft depending on how much of the previous build survives. Our Vasant Kunj renovation guide prices it scope by scope.

DDA flats give location at the lowest cost per sq ft in South West Delhi, with renovation the unlock: a Rs 2.8 crore flat plus a Rs 45 lakh renovation routinely out-lives a Rs 5 crore floor on both comfort and value. Private floors give you ownership depth, parking and freedom to rebuild vertically. The decision is budget and horizon, not taste.

For the right buyer, yes: farmhouses along the Mahipalpur and Kapashera edge give South Delhi land at a fraction of Sainik Farm or Chattarpur rates, with the airport fifteen minutes away. Due diligence is everything here: land use, boundaries and access decide whether the plot is a home or a lawsuit. Have both a lawyer and a designer walk it before money moves.

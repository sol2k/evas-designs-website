The renovation lever

Tired stock in every one of these bands trades at a 20 to 35 percent discount to rebuilt or new stock, and a full renovation costs 15 to 20 percent of the new price. Buy tired, renovate well, hold long: it is the most repeatable arbitrage in Delhi NCR housing.

Interiors are the second budget

Whatever you buy, the home you live in is the purchase plus the interior. Budget Rs 2,150 to 3,000 per sq ft for a full premium interior or Rs 950 to 1,400 for a selective upgrade, and review the floor plan before you sign, not after.

07 Asked by every family we meet

Your questions, answered plainly.

For most families, South Delhi around Greater Kailash, Malviya Nagar and Vasant Kunj wins on schools, parks, hospitals and daily convenience. Dwarka suits families priced out of South Delhi who still want planned sectors and metro access, and Rohini is the practical pick in North West Delhi with strong schools and its own retail hubs.

As a working rule: Rs 1 to 2 crore in Rohini or Dwarka, Rs 2.5 to 4.5 crore in Malviya Nagar or Vasant Kunj, and Rs 4 to 8 crore in Greater Kailash depending on block and floor quality. South Delhi builder floors carry wide quality spreads, so the same budget can buy either a tired 1980s floor or a newly rebuilt one with lift and stilt parking.

Greater Kailash, Malviya Nagar and Vasant Kunj score highest on walkability, hospital proximity and quiet, established streets. Dwarka performs well within its sectors, particularly near sector markets and metro stations. In all three South Delhi areas, single-storey living or a lift is the detail that matters most, which is why rebuilt builder floors have become the default senior-friendly format.

Builder floors give you ownership of the plot share, faster customization and modern specifications, at Rs 3 to 8 crore in South Delhi. DDA and cooperative flats cost far less, typically Rs 1.2 to 3 crore in better locations, but carry older services, no lift in many blocks and renovation ceilings imposed by structure. Our renovation cost guide for Delhi NCR prices both paths.

Dwarka leads on sheer density of Blue Line stations. South Delhi is served by the Yellow and Magenta lines through Malviya Nagar, Hauz Khas and Vasant Vihar, and the Airport Express through Dhaula Kuan serves Vasant Kunj indirectly. Rohini connects on the Red Line, and the Aerocity belt is a ten-minute hop from the Airport Express.

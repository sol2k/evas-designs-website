02 · Fees Before you sign anything

What an architect costs on Golf Course Extension Road in 2026.

Architect fees on this corridor are quoted a dozen different ways. These are honest working ranges, so you can compare any quote you receive on equal terms, including ours.

01

Lump-sum design fees · Rs 60,000 to 3,00,000

Common for straightforward builder floors and compact plots: floor plans, elevations, working drawings and one or two revisions for a fixed price. Higher end covers stilt-plus-four structures and structural drawings.

02

Per-sq-ft rates · Rs 30 to 120 per sq ft

Some Gurugram studios quote per square foot of built-up area instead. On a 1,800 sq ft floor this works out similar to a lump sum, but always confirm whether 3D views, liaison and site visits are included or billed separately.

03

Percentage of build cost · 2 to 5 percent

The traditional model for architect-led projects: the fee scales with what the home will actually cost to build. Often the fairest structure for villas and complex plots, since it aligns the architect's incentive with your budget.

04

Design-build, folded into one contract

Studios such as Evas Designs often fold design into the overall project cost instead of charging a separate fee. You lose the ability to compare design fees line by line, but you gain one accountable party for both the drawing and the building.

These are indicative ranges for budgeting conversations, not quotes. Complexity, plot conditions and the scope of liaison work all move the number. For a real figure, the only honest route is a site visit and a written scope, which is exactly how we prepare ours.

03 · Method Use this on any architect

Four checks that separate a good architect from a good portfolio.

Apply these to every studio on this list, including Evas Designs. An architect who answers all four confidently will rarely disappoint you.

01

Do they walk sites on this corridor?

An architect who is on Golf Course Extension Road every week already knows the norms, the vendors and the approval quirks. Ask how many active sites they have within two kilometres of your plot.

02

Visit a two-year-old project

Fresh renders flatter everyone. A completed home you can walk through tells you how the plans actually aged: how the staircase lands, how the light falls at 4 pm, whether the detailing survived contact with a real contractor.

03

Read the scope, not just the fee

Two quotes at Rs 1,00,000 can cover wildly different work. Check whether 3D elevations, structural drawings, liaison with authorities and site visit frequency are written down, or left politely vague.

04

Ask who actually draws your project

Some studios hand sold projects to junior draftsmen after the partner wins the meeting. Ask directly: whose name goes on your drawings, and who do you call when a wall location needs to move mid-build?

Worked example

A 5,000 sq ft office on Sohna Road at standard renovation grade, Rs 1,650 per sq ft, lands at about Rs 82 lakh all-in including deep cleaning and weekend working premiums. The same floor relocated to a new building would cost four to six times that in deposits, fit-out and downtime.

What moves the number

Glass cabin ratio, workstation density, AV load, after-hours working and building access rules move quotes by up to thirty percent. Two offices of identical size can legitimately quote fifty lakh apart; the audit is in the line items, which we provide open-book.

04 · The Process How a commercial renovation runs

Four stages, one accountable team.

Commercial clients do not buy interiors; they buy certainty about dates, budgets and business continuity. This is the sequence every Evas commercial renovation follows, and where the checkpoints sit.

01

Survey and scope, 3 to 5 days

Site measurement, services audit, landlord norm check and an operations interview with your admin team. You receive a line-item scope with a fixed price and a written timeline, not a rate card with asterisks.

02

Approvals and mobilisation, 1 to 2 weeks

Tower management and landlord approvals, fire NOC coordination where needed, material lock-in at contract rates, and crew scheduling around your working calendar including night and weekend windows.

03

Phased execution, 6 to 14 weeks

Zoned demolition, then parallel civil, MEP, ceiling and joinery trades with weekly photo reports and a fixed review call. Noisy trades stay in after-hours slots; your team keeps working on the rest of the floor.

04

Deep clean and handover, 3 to 5 days

Professional deep cleaning, marble crystallisation, air purge, snag rectification and a joint walkthrough against the original scope. Handover means your staff can sit and work the same morning, not after a week of cleaning.

The finished product

Deep-cleaned, lit and stocked. Not handed over, delivered.

The last five days of every commercial renovation are the ones clients remember. Marble is crystallised, glass is polished, carpets are extraction-cleaned, and the air is purged of construction dust before a single carton of your equipment enters. Your team walks into a floor that smells like a new building, because for practical purposes it is one.

06 · FAQ What commercial tenants ask first

Commercial renovation in Gurgaon, answered plainly.

A light refresh of an existing office, repainting, carpet, lighting and minor joinery, runs Rs 950 to 1,400 per sq ft. A full office renovation with new ceilings, partitions, workstations and services runs Rs 1,400 to 2,500 per sq ft depending on specification. A 5,000 sq ft corporate floor at a premium scope therefore costs roughly Rs 70 lakh to 1.25 crore, executed over eight to fourteen weeks, mostly outside your business hours if the office stays operational.

A retail showroom of 2,000 to 3,000 sq ft takes six to ten weeks. A corporate office floor of 5,000 to 10,000 sq ft takes eight to fourteen weeks. Restaurants and cafes with heavy services run ten to sixteen weeks because MEP and kitchen approvals add stages. We phase work so trading offices can keep operating through most of the schedule, with noisy trades boxed into weekends and nights.

Yes, and most of our commercial renovations in Gurgaon work exactly this way. We divide the floor into zones, hand over one zone at a time, run demolition and drilling after hours, and maintain dust barriers and separate access throughout. It adds roughly ten to fifteen percent to the timeline but avoids the far larger cost of relocating staff for three months.

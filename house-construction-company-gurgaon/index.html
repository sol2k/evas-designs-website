01: What a company covers Company vs bare contractor

What a house construction company covers, that a bare contractor does not.

The word "construction" hides seven different jobs. Here is each one, and who carries it in each delivery model.

Inclusion What we deliver Bare contractor
Plot assessment and floor rights Written verification of buildable floors, FAR and setbacks for your plot before design Not included: assumes your drawings are final
Architecture and structural design Plans, 3D elevations and RCC design by in-house architects and engineers You hire separately, then reconcile disagreements on site
Government approvals MCG, DTCP or HSVP sanction liaison, fire NOC, completion and occupancy paperwork Owner's responsibility, often discovered late
RCC construction and masonry Foundation to slab with cube tests and steel checks logged per pour Included, quality varies with supervision
Services: electrical, plumbing, HVAC provision Designed in, not retrofitted: points, panels, pressure and drainage planned with structure Executed per drawing, coordination gaps common
Interior design and execution In-house interior designers deliver modular kitchen, wardrobes, false ceilings and styling You hire an interior designer after handover and re-explain everything
Handover and defect liability Snagging, deep clean, utility connections, written defect liability period Keys handed over, retention rarely defined

The economics: hiring architect, contractor and interior designer separately layers a margin at every handoff. Our single-contract builds typically run 8 to 12 percent cheaper for the same specification, with one accountable party when anything goes wrong.

03: How we build The Evas delivery process

Assess and design

Plot verification, brief, plans and photoreal 3D elevations. You approve the home visually before sanction, not after walls are up.

02

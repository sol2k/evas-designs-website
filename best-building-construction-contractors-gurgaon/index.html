01: Contractor types Know what you are actually buying

The four contractor types in Gurgaon, and what each really costs.

Most building disputes start with a mismatch between the type of contractor hired and the owner's actual availability to manage the site.

Contractor type What they do 2026 rate Best suited for
Labour contractor Supply masons, steel fixers, carpenters and helpers; you buy all materials ₹280 to 420 per sq ft, materials extra Owners who can be on site daily with a trusted engineer
Full contractor Build from your architect's drawings with materials included; you handle approvals and interiors ₹1,650 to 2,800 per sq ft, luxury higher Owners with completed drawings and their own project manager
Project management consultant Supervise and audit the contractor on your behalf; build nothing themselves ₹45 to 90 per sq ft of supervision fee Large or luxury builds where quality control justifies the extra layer
Design-build company Design, engineering, approvals, construction and interiors under one contract and one BOQ ₹2,200 to 5,500 plus per sq ft turnkey Most plot owners: one accountability, no gaps between firms

Rule of thumb: if you cannot visit the site three to four times a week with a competent engineer, the labour-contractor route will cost you more than it saves. Design-build exists precisely for owners who want one accountable party instead of four.

03: Scope and materials Work scope and material specifications

The BOQ lines and material specs your contract must name.

A contract is only as good as its bill of quantities. These are the specifications we write into every Evas BOQ, and the ones you should demand from any contractor.

Element What to specify Why it matters
Cement OPC 43 or 53 grade, branded (Ultratech, ACC, Ambuja or equivalent) Lower grades or loose cement are the most common silent downgrades
Steel Fe500 or Fe550 with mill test certificates, branded (Tata, JSW, SAIL) Uncertified rebar fails ductility tests and corrodes faster
Concrete Design mix by element: typically M25 to M30 slabs, M20 to M25 columns Cube-test records prove the poured mix, not the promised one
Walls Fly-ash bricks or AAC blocks with mortar mix ratio stated Block quality decides cracking and thermal performance
Waterproofing Branded system (Dr Fixit, Fosroc or equivalent) with written warranty The single most litigated defect in Delhi NCR homes
Electrical Wire brand and gauge (Polycab, Havells), MCB brand, earthing standard Unsafe wiring is invisible after plaster and expensive to redo
Plumbing CPVC or PPR pipe brand and class (Ashirvad, Astral, Prince) Buried leaks destroy ceilings and builder-floor relations alike
Finishes Tile brand and size, paint brand and coats, door and window profiles Finish downgrades are how cheap BOQs win bids then lose trust

Work scope clarity: state in writing whether approvals, borewell, rainwater harvesting, STP, facade, lift, modular kitchen, wardrobes and landscaping are in scope or excluded. The most common Gurgaon dispute is not price: it is an item both parties assumed the other was covering.

04: Supervision and payments Site supervision and payment schedules

How to verify supervision, and structure payments so you keep leverage.

Supervision and payment structure decide whether your project stays honest. Here is the framework we recommend on every build we deliver.

Site supervision: the three proofs

  • Named resident engineer: one person assigned to your site, not a floating supervisor across ten projects
  • Written weekly reports: progress photos, labour count, material deliveries and test records, every week without being asked
  • Unannounced visits: ask to see two live sites today; serious contractors say yes immediately
Payment event Fair share Red flag
Mobilisation advance Up to 10 percent Anything above 15 percent, or cash with no written receipt
Foundation and plinth complete 15 to 20 percent cumulative Payment demanded before excavation is verifiably done
Each RCC slab cast and cured 15 to 20 percent per floor, verified Calendar-based monthly demands with no stage check
Brickwork, plaster and services 20 to 25 percent cumulative Extra-item rates left blank in the contract
Finishes and joinery 15 to 20 percent cumulative Full payment demanded before final finishes start
Snagging closure and handover 5 to 10 percent retained till closure No retention: you lose all leverage at the finish line

08: FAQ Contractor questions from plot owners

Choosing a contractor in Gurgaon, answered directly.

The eight questions owners ask us before awarding a construction contract.

For a full turnkey build, Evas Designs ranks first among Gurgaon's local contractors because design, engineering, approvals, construction and interiors sit under one contract with one itemised BOQ, in-house resident engineers on every site and a 4.9-star record across 180 plus projects since 2015. For budget-first standard builds, local firms such as Nitara Projects, Consyard Homes and 3S Buildcon are credible alternatives to compare on BOQ detail, supervision model and handover record.

Four main types: labour contractors who only supply workers and leave materials to you; full contractors who build from your architect's drawings with you coordinating approvals and interiors; project management consultants who supervise on your behalf but build nothing themselves; and design-build companies that carry design, approvals, construction and interiors under one contract. Most budget overruns happen in the gaps between the second model's separate parties, which is why plot owners increasingly choose design-build.

Labour-contractor rates run ₹280 to 420 per sq ft with materials extra. Full construction contracts cost ₹1,650 to 1,900 per sq ft for standard specifications, ₹2,200 to 2,800 for premium, and ₹3,200 to 5,500 plus for luxury homes. Anything quoted far below these bands usually means specification downgrade hidden in the BOQ, so compare itemised BOQs rather than headline rates.

Milestone-linked, never calendar-linked: roughly 10 percent mobilisation advance against a bank guarantee or materials-on-site, then payments tied to verifiable stages such as foundation, each floor slab, brickwork, plaster, and finishes, with the last 5 to 10 percent held until snagging and handover. Refuse any contractor demanding more than 15 percent before work starts, and pay each milestone only after your engineer verifies the stage.

Ask for three things before signing: the name and experience of the resident site engineer assigned to you, sample weekly reports with photographs from a current project, and permission to visit two live sites unannounced. Weak contractors promise supervision but run ten sites with one floating supervisor; serious firms assign a dedicated engineer per project and log concrete cube tests and steel checks in writing.

At minimum: cement grade and brand, steel grade with manufacturer, concrete mix per element, brick or block type, waterproofing system and warranty, wire and cable brand with gauge, plumbing pipe brand and class, and sanitaryware and fitting brands with model numbers. A BOQ that says only 'as per standard' is unenforceable; a BOQ with brands and grades is a contract you can hold the contractor to.

On paper, often 10 to 15 percent cheaper, because you buy materials directly. In practice, most owners without construction experience lose that saving to material wastage, wrong quantities, idle labour and rework. If you cannot be on site daily with a trusted engineer, the labour-contractor route usually costs more by the time keys are handed over.

Eight: show an itemised BOQ from a completed project; who is my resident engineer; what is your milestone payment schedule; what material brands and grades will you use; how do you handle extra items and rate escalation; what is the written timeline and remedy for delay; what defect liability do you offer after handover; and can I visit two live sites today. A contractor who answers all eight confidently is worth shortlisting.

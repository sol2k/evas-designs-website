01 · The Field Guide The five house types of Phase 3

Walk any Phase 3 block. You will see all five.

Each type has its own plan, its own risk and its own budget. Find yours below.

01

The original DLF house

Small rooms, thick walls, a plot that has carried two generations. These houses reward a deep renovation: open the plan, keep the bones. Budget Rs 2,500 to 3,500 per sq ft. Full rebuilds make sense only when the structure has given up.

02

The stilt-plus-two floor

The workhorse of Phase 3. Parking below, two floors above. The common ask is one more floor or a facade refresh. First question is always structural: was the frame designed to grow? Check that before you dream.

03

The redeveloped plot

An old house came down. A new floor or two went up. If you own one, interiors and services are your project now. If you are buying one, read the column grid before you sign, and check what the sanction actually shows.

04

The corner plot house

Double frontage, more light, more rules. Setbacks run longer on two sides, and the design must answer two streets. Done well, a corner plot becomes the best house on the block. It rarely forgives a lazy plan.

05

The investor floor

Built to rent, finished to a price. If you hold one, a mid-life upgrade, better services, honest materials, lifts it into a higher rental band for less than a new purchase costs. If you live next to one, you already know which it is.

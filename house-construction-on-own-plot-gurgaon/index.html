02: Approvals Government criteria and compliance

Approvals your plot build needs before a single brick.

Own-plot construction in Gurgaon is heavily rule-bound, and that protects you: a sanctioned, completed, certified house is worth far more at resale than one with regularisation problems.

Approval or rule Who issues it Why it matters
Building plan sanction MCG for municipal areas, DTCP or HSVP for licensed colonies Construction without it is illegal and can be ordered demolished
Floor space index and height limits Set in the colony licence and Haryana building codes Decides how many floors and how much area your plot can legally carry
Completion certificate The same sanctioning authority, after site inspection Confirms the built house matches the sanctioned drawings
Occupancy certificate Sanctioning authority, post completion Legal permission to live in the house and to get permanent power and water connections
Labour and site compliance Contractor responsibility under Haryana labour and safety norms Protects you from liability for site accidents and unauthorised labour
  • Floor rights scale with plot size: roughly stilt plus two floors on plots up to 150 sq yd, stilt plus three up to 250 sq yd, and stilt plus four up to 350 sq yd, subject to FAR and height limits.
  • Setbacks and coverage matter: your buildable footprint shrinks with plot width, which is why two plots of the same size can have very different floor plans.
  • Stilt parking is usually mandatory on smaller plots and counts differently from habitable floors.
  • We confirm your exact entitlement first: a written plot assessment before design starts, so drawings never need a redo at the sanction desk.

How long a plot build takes, stage by stage.

A premium stilt-plus-three home of 4,000 to 5,000 sq ft runs 12 to 16 months from sanction to handover. Here is where the time goes.

Stage Duration What happens
Design and sanction 8 to 12 weeks Brief, plans, 3D elevations, structural drawings, submission to MCG or DTCP, liaison till approval
Excavation and foundation 3 to 5 weeks Soil check, excavation, footings, plinth beam, anti-termite treatment
RCC structure 3 to 4 months Columns, beams, slabs floor by floor, with cube tests and site engineer sign-off at every pour
Masonry and plaster 6 to 8 weeks Brickwork, lintels, internal and external plaster, waterproofing of wet areas and terrace
Services 6 to 8 weeks, overlaps Electrical conduits and panels, plumbing, HVAC provision, low voltage and automation
Finishes and joinery 3 to 4 months Flooring, doors and windows, modular kitchen and wardrobes, false ceilings, paint, facade
Handover 2 to 3 weeks Snagging, testing, deep clean, completion certificate filing, utility connections, key handover

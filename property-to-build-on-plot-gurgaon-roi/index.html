What to build on your plot in Gurgaon for maximum ROI?

Quick answer: on a residential plot in Gurgaon, co-living PGs and coworking assets return the highest yields in 2026, roughly 8 to 13 percent gross per year, against 3 to 4.5 percent for conventional builder-floor rentals. A stilt-plus-three PG of 14 to 16 beds in Sectors 43 to 57 earns about ₹1.4 to 2 lakh a month; a basement-plus-ground coworking floor of 40 seats earns ₹2.5 to 4 lakh; and a hybrid of coworking below with residential floors above is this year's most asked-about configuration. Builder floors remain the lowest-effort asset with strong capital appreciation on sale. Below: real 2026 numbers for every property type, the rules that decide what your plot may carry, and how to get a free ROI assessment of your specific plot.

02: PG deep dive Co-living economics

What a 14-bed PG actually earns.

Take a 250 sq yd plot in Sectors 43 to 57, build stilt plus three floors of about 3,500 sq ft, and fit it as a co-living PG. Construction at premium rates runs about ₹77 to 98 lakh, the PG fit-out another ₹25 to 35 lakh, and furniture ₹8 to 12 lakh: roughly ₹1.1 to 1.4 crore ready to lease.

  • Occupancy drives everything: at 90 percent occupancy the asset grosses ₹15 to 20 lakh a year; at 70 percent it struggles to cover costs.
  • Operating costs run 35 to 40 percent of collections: food if included, housekeeping, power, internet, staff and platform commissions.
  • Location beats specification: beds within 15 minutes of Cyber City, Udyog Vihar or a metro station fill first and command the top of the rent band.
  • Design for revenue: an extra rentable bed is worth ₹1.2 to 1.9 lakh a year, so layouts are planned around bed count first, aesthetics second. That is exactly how Evas Designs approaches PG architecture.
03: The hybrid model Coworking below, living above

Coworking in the basement and ground, home on top.

The strongest plot play in Gurgaon right now is mixed-use: a coworking floor on the basement and ground where commercial demand lives, and premium residential floors above where capital appreciation lives. Dedicated desks across Gurgaon rent at ₹6,500 to 10,000 per seat a month, and neighbourhood coworking near metro corridors runs at 85 percent-plus occupancy.

05: FAQ Plot investment questions

PG, coworking and builder floors, answered directly.

The eight questions plot owners ask us before choosing what to build.

Co-living PGs and coworking assets currently return the highest gross yields on residential plots in Gurgaon: 8 to 12 percent per year against 3 to 4.5 percent for conventional builder-floor rentals. A stilt-plus-three PG of 14 to 16 beds in Sectors 43 to 57 typically earns ₹1.4 to 2 lakh a month, and a 35 to 45 seat basement-plus-ground coworking floor earns ₹2.5 to 4 lakh a month. The trade-off is operating effort: PGs and coworking are businesses, not passive rent.

Running paying guests is a commercial activity, so compliance matters: many colonies require a change-of-use or commercial billing for large PGs, a fire NOC is mandatory once occupancy crosses the threshold set for guest accommodation, and Haryana requires trade-licence and GST registration once you operate commercially. In practice, small family-style PGs operate quietly across Gurgaon while institutional operators register properly. We advise doing it by the book from day one, because a licensed PG is also a bankable, sellable asset.

Yes, this is one of the strongest hybrid models on Gurgaon plots: basement plus ground as a 30 to 45 seat coworking floor, upper floors as your residence or rental apartments. Check three things first: your colony licence's commercial-use clause, parking adequacy under MCG rules, and fire clearance for the occupancy load. Corner plots on 12 metre-plus roads in Sectors 44 to 57 are the sweet spot, close to metro corridors and office hubs.

Structure and finishes cost the same as a premium home, roughly ₹2,200 to 2,800 per sq ft, and the PG-specific fit-out adds ₹700 to 1,000 per sq ft: partition-ready layouts, two kitchens, laundry, fire systems, CCTV, access control and furniture. A 250 sq yd stilt-plus-three plot carrying about 3,500 sq ft of PG area therefore lands near ₹1.05 to 1.3 crore all-in, ready to lease. Furnished beds in this segment rent at ₹9,000 to 16,000 per month depending on sharing and sector.

Builder floors are the lower-effort, lower-yield asset: build once, rent or sell each floor, and earn 3 to 4.5 percent gross on rent with strong capital appreciation on sale. A PG is a business that roughly doubles or triples the rental yield but demands operations, staff and compliance. Many of our clients now build hybrid: coworking on the lower floors for cash flow, builder floors above for capital growth, which is why mixed-use plots in Sectors 44 to 57 are the most asked-about configuration this year.

Commercial accommodation and coworking services are both taxable supplies: GST registration is required once turnover crosses the threshold, coworking typically charges 18 percent GST on memberships, and PG operators above the threshold must register and charge accordingly. Rental income from a plain residential letting to a tenant for residence is taxed differently and is outside GST. Factor registration, invoicing and annual filing into your operating cost, roughly 1 to 2 percent of collections.

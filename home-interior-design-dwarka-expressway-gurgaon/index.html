Your project not listed?

The expressway adds launches every quarter. If your tower is not in the table, send the name on WhatsApp; we have likely already measured a unit there or can pull the tower drawings before your site visit.

New launch? Move at booking stage

On towers still under construction, the cheapest interior is decided months before possession. Beam positions, duct runs and kitchen walls are locked early; a design review at booking stage is free and routinely saves Rs 5 to 10 lakh in avoided civil rework.

Ready tower? The upgrade cycle is now

Most ready towers here are five to twelve years old, exactly when kitchens, wardrobes and bathroom fittings reach the end of their comfortable life. A selective upgrade costs a fraction of a full interior and lifts resale and rental value together.

What the rate includes

Civil changes, flooring, false ceilings, electrical rewiring where needed, plumbing, panelling, kitchens, wardrobes, painting, polish, styling, and a post-work deep clean before handover. One number, one contract, no per-room arithmetic.

What it excludes, honestly

Loose furniture you already own, appliances, RWA fit-out deposits and any society charges. All four are listed in writing before you sign, so the final invoice matches the estimate.

Why ranges, not a package price

Expressway towers differ: a 4,000 sq ft Sobha International City villa and a 1,500 sq ft Emaar Gurgaon Greens flat are both "homes on Dwarka Expressway" but carry very different scope. We price from your measured plan, never from a brochure package.

Design for this corridor

Homes here face the expressway. The design should own that.

Dwarka Expressway apartments have a signature condition most designers ignore: long facades along a wide, fast road. That means light is abundant but so is dust and noise, and the window wall dominates every living room. Our corridor designs treat the facade as the asset: seating oriented to the view, layered drapery for glare and acoustic glass upgrades, and entry foyers that buffer dust before it reaches your interiors.

  • Living rooms planned around the expressway-facing window wall
  • Acoustic and dust-buffering upgrades within society norms
  • Foyer-led plans with shoe and coat storage at the entry

08 · FAQ Asked at every site visit on the corridor

Home interiors on Dwarka Expressway, answered plainly.

For a full luxury interior of a new apartment, budget Rs 2,150 to 3,000 per sq ft of carpet area. That works out to roughly Rs 32 to 45 lakh for a 2BHK of 1,400 to 1,800 sq ft, Rs 45 to 65 lakh for a 3BHK of 2,000 to 2,600 sq ft, and Rs 65 lakh to 1.1 crore for a 4BHK of 3,000 sq ft and above. A lighter refresh of a lived-in home, with paint, lighting and soft furnishings, runs Rs 450 to 700 per sq ft.

Sectors 102, 104, 106, 108, 112 and 113 hold the largest ready-to-move stock: Emaar Gurgaon Greens, Hero Homes and Indiabulls Heights around 102 and 104, Sobha Altus, Godrej The Meridien and Sobha City around 106 to 108, and Tata Gurgaon Gateway, Experion Windchants, Tata La Vida and M3M Capital around 112 and 113. Most of these are five to twelve years old, which is exactly when kitchens, wardrobes and bathroom fittings reach their upgrade cycle.

A full luxury interior of a 3BHK takes 14 to 18 weeks from design freeze, and a 4BHK takes 16 to 24 weeks. A selective upgrade such as kitchen plus two bathrooms and wardrobes runs 8 to 12 weeks, and a refresh is four to six weeks. We commit these dates in writing at contract stage and send a weekly photo report so you can track progress from your phone.

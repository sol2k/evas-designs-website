02 · Cost factors Why quotes spread

The six factors that decide where in the band you land.

Two projects with the same floor plan can quote Rs 5 lakh apart for honest reasons. These are the six, in the order they actually move money.

  • Carpet area and efficiency. A 1,150 sq ft efficient plan interiors cheaper than a 1,200 sq ft choppy one. Hallways, shafts and odd corners are joinery cost with no storage return.
  • Kitchen share of budget. The kitchen eats 25 to 35 percent of a 2BHK interior budget; shutter material and hardware drive it more than size does.
  • Wardrobe count and internals. Two bedrooms means two to three wardrobes; each additional internal accessory set (trays, mirrors, safes, lighting) adds Rs 15,000 to 40,000.
  • False ceiling extent. Full-home gypsum ceilings with cove lighting add Rs 90,000 to 2 lakh over a plain POP-and-paint ceiling; selective ceilings in living and master save 40 percent of that.
  • Lighting design. A layered lighting plan (coves, spots, pendants, profiles) costs Rs 60,000 to 1.8 lakh but is the single biggest visual upgrade per rupee.
  • Site condition. A bare-shell handover needs electrical and plumbing additions; a resale 2BHK may need civil repair before joinery. We survey before quoting either way.

Live rules · Delhi NCR winter 2026-27

Is construction allowed in Delhi today? The honest two-minute answer.

This question gets typed into Google lakhs of times every winter because the answer genuinely changes week to week. Construction in Delhi NCR is governed by GRAP, the graded response that tightens as air quality worsens, and by special CAQM orders that can stop work for fixed windows. As of late November 2026 the season's bans have been lifted: GRAP Stage III was revoked on 17 November 2026 and the remaining stage curbs were withdrawn by 23 November as air quality improved, so normal construction is permitted with standard dust-control obligations. That status will tighten again when AQI worsens. This page gives you three things: how to check the live order yourself in two minutes, exactly what each GRAP stage bans and permits, and how we plan projects so the winter costs you weeks of schedule rather than months of chaos.

Quick answer: As of 26 November 2026, construction is allowed in Delhi NCR: CAQM revoked GRAP Stage III on 17 November and withdrew the remaining stage curbs by 23 November 2026 as AQI improved, though standard dust-control rules apply on every site year-round. The status changes with air quality, so before mobilising labour check the same-day CAQM order on caqm.nic.in: Stage I and II mean build with strict dust control, Stage III bans most construction and demolition except critical public projects, and Stage IV adds truck-entry and activity bans on top.

Special orders sit outside the ladder

CAQM can impose fixed-window curbs regardless of the stage, as it did with the 5 to 20 November 2026 construction restrictions across NCR. These orders override the stage table for their window, which is why checking the latest order matters more than knowing the ladder.

Interior work is treated differently

The bans target dust-generating construction and demolition. Interior finishing, carpentry, painting, false ceilings, electrical and modular installation inside an existing structure is not the target, and we keep those crews productive through most restriction windows. 03 · Planning around it Never fully stopped

The work that continues, and the calendar smart projects follow.

A well-run Delhi NCR project is never 100 percent halted by GRAP, because the schedule is built so dusty and clean phases alternate with the season.

  • Interior finishing. Painting, polishing, false ceilings, carpentry and modular installs proceed inside a closed structure in every stage. Painting and false ceiling work are the classic winter slots.
  • Fabrication off-site. Wardrobes, kitchen carcasses, metalwork and stone cutting happen in workshops unaffected by site bans, then install in days.
  • Design and approvals. Sanction drawings, plan approvals, 3D design and procurement decisions are winter-proof work that saves spring weeks.
  • Procurement. Materials ordered during restrictions arrive exactly when the site reopens, instead of queueing behind everyone else's spring orders.
  • Structural work in open windows. Pours and masonry are sequenced into allowed periods with labour and machinery pre-mobilised, so no allowed week is wasted.

The NCR building calendar

  • October to January: restriction season; plan interiors, approvals and procurement
  • February to April: prime structure and exterior season; book crews early
  • May to June: work continues; heat management for labour and curing
  • July to September: monsoon slows outdoor and excavation work; interiors continue

What a GRAP-ready contract includes

  • A re-sequencing clause that moves interior phases into ban windows automatically
  • Dust-control line items priced honestly, not buried or skipped
  • Labour remobilisation terms so crews return within days, not weeks
  • Weekly schedule updates tied to the live CAQM order

03 — Old houses & bungalows Gurgaon & South Delhi specifics

Renovating an old house or bungalow is a different sport.

The houses worth renovating in Gurgaon and South Delhi are usually 20–40 years old — great plots, tired buildings. That changes both the scope and the sequence.

What old-house renovation really involves

A 30-year-old bungalow in DLF or a South Delhi kothi rarely needs just "new interiors." Once we open it up, the honest scope usually includes structural strengthening, complete rewiring and re-plumbing, terrace and basement waterproofing, damp and termite treatment, and a facade that matches what the interiors have become.

  • Structure first — columns, beams and slabs assessed before a single design decision; strengthening adds ₹300–600 per sq ft where needed
  • Services replaced, not patched — full electrical and CPVC plumbing replacement in one go, while walls are open
  • Waterproofing is non-negotiable — terraces, basements and bathroom sunk slabs; the #1 source of post-renovation disputes
  • Facade as value — a redesigned elevation lifts resale and rental value disproportionately in Gurgaon's plotted colonies

Budget guide: a 3,000 sq ft bungalow renovation in Gurgaon lands between ₹60 lakh and ₹1.1 crore depending on structural and facade scope — ₹2,000–3,500 per sq ft, against ₹2,900–4,500+ to demolish and rebuild.

04 — The big decision Renovation vs reconstruction

Renovate or rebuild? The 70% rule.

The most expensive renovation mistake is renovating a house that should have been rebuilt. Here's the math we run with clients.

Factor Renovation Demolish & rebuild
Cost per sq ft ₹2,200 – 3,500 (premium scope) ₹2,900 – 4,500+ (construction only)
Timeline 3 – 7 months 12 – 20 months
Approvals Usually none for internal work Fresh sanctioned plans, demolition NOC, neighbour consents
Structure Works if the frame is sound; strengthening possible New frame designed for any floor count or layout
Layout freedom Limited by existing columns and walls Complete — stilt parking, lifts, double heights, setbacks
You should choose it when… Frame is healthy and renovation quote is under ~70% of rebuild cost Frame is compromised, you want more floors, or quotes cross 70%

The 70% rule: when an honest renovation quote crosses roughly 70% of what reconstruction would cost, rebuilding wins — you get a brand-new structure, modern span design and full layout freedom for marginally more money. Renovation risk lives in the civil layer — the wall you want to move, the bathroom slab that needs re-waterproofing, the wiring from 1998. When interiors and civil work belong to different companies, every surprise becomes a negotiation about whose fault it is. When one team owns both, surprises just get solved.

06 — Budgeting right Before you collect quotes

How to plan a renovation budget that survives contact with reality.

Four steps that separate a controlled renovation from a runaway one.

01

Get a physical inspection before any number

Any quote given without opening up your walls, checking the terrace and testing the wiring is a guess. Structure, damp and services decide 30% of an old-house budget — insist on a site visit first.

02

Budget rooms, not just square feet

Price the kitchen and bathrooms first — they carry 40–50% of a standard renovation. Whatever remains after them is your real budget for everything else.

03

Hold a 10–15% discovery contingency

Old houses hide things: corroded pipes, dead wiring, damp behind dado tiles, termite trails. A contingency turns discoveries into decisions instead of disputes.

04

Time it around the season

Demolition and masonry face winter GRAP restrictions in Delhi NCR — starting civil work in September–October or February–March keeps your calendar honest.

07 — FAQ Renovation cost questions

Renovation costs in NCR, answered plainly.

What homeowners ask us before starting a renovation in Delhi NCR and Gurgaon.

In 2026, house renovation in Delhi NCR and Gurgaon costs roughly ₹900–1,400 per sq ft for a cosmetic refresh (paint, polish, minor repairs), ₹1,400–2,200 per sq ft for a standard renovation (new bathrooms, kitchen, flooring, electricals), and ₹2,200–3,500+ per sq ft for a premium or structural renovation (layout changes, facade work, designer finishes). Gurgaon sits at the upper half of these bands because of higher labour rates and premium material expectations.

A typical 3BHK of 1,400–1,800 sq ft costs ₹14–25 lakh for a standard renovation — new kitchen, 2–3 bathrooms, flooring, painting, electrical upgrades and wardrobes. A cosmetic refresh of the same flat runs ₹12–18 lakh less, while a premium renovation with layout changes, designer bathrooms and full interior design runs ₹35–55 lakh. The kitchen and bathrooms usually account for 40–50% of the total budget.

Bungalow and villa renovation in Gurgaon typically costs ₹2,000–3,500 per sq ft because the scope is wider than a flat: facade redesign, waterproofing of terraces and basements, structural strengthening, staircase and double-height modifications, landscaping and boundary walls. A 3,000 sq ft bungalow renovation usually lands between ₹60 lakh and ₹1.1 crore depending on how much structural and facade work is involved.

Renovation is cheaper when the structure is sound — a full premium renovation costs ₹2,200–3,500 per sq ft against ₹2,900–4,500+ per sq ft for demolition and reconstruction, plus 8–14 months of extra time and fresh approvals. Rebuild when the foundation or frame is compromised, when you want extra floors the old structure cannot take, or when renovation quotes cross roughly 70% of reconstruction cost — beyond that point, rebuilding gives you a new asset for marginally more.

Livspace and HomeLane are primarily interior-furnishing companies built around factory-made modular packages — they excel at kitchens and wardrobes but subcontract civil work like breaking walls, masonry, waterproofing and structural changes, which is where renovation risk actually lives. A design-build studio like Evas Designs handles the architecture, structural work and interiors with one in-house team, so the civil and design sides cannot blame each other when something goes wrong, and the design is drawn around your house rather than pulled from a catalogue.

A cosmetic refresh takes 3–6 weeks. A standard full-home renovation of a flat takes 8–14 weeks; a bungalow or villa renovation with structural, facade or waterproofing scope takes 4–7 months. In Delhi NCR, add buffer for winter — GRAP restrictions between November and January can pause dust-generating work like demolition, masonry and tile cutting for days to weeks at a stretch.

The five costs that surprise homeowners most: structural repairs discovered after demolition (₹50,000–3 lakh), complete electrical rewiring and panel upgrade in 20+ year old houses (₹1.5–3 lakh), plumbing replacement with new CPVC lines (₹80,000–2 lakh), termite and damp treatment (₹30,000–1 lakh), and society or RWA permissions, debris removal and lift protection in apartments (₹20,000–60,000). A renovation quote without a physical site inspection almost always misses these.

Internal, non-structural renovation needs no municipal approval. You need permissions when you change the structure — adding or removing load-bearing elements, extending covered area, adding a floor, or altering the facade setback — which requires sanctioned revised plans from MCD, HSVP or the relevant authority. Apartments additionally need RWA or society NOC before work starts, and most Gurgaon condominiums have fixed working hours and debris rules that your contractor must follow.

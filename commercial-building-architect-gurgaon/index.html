What moves the cost

Services (HVAC, electrical load, data), floor loading for display or equipment, acoustics, and the approval scope set the final number far more than the veneer does. Two showrooms of the same size can differ by 60 percent on these alone.

What moves the timeline

Estate and landlord drawing approvals in DLF, M3M and corporate parks add 2 to 6 weeks before site work; fire NOC for assembly spaces adds more. We start the approval chain in parallel with design freeze, not after it.

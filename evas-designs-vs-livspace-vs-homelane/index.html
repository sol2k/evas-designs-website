Where the saving comes from

No franchise fee, no experience-centre rent, no sales commissions, and no subcontractor margin. The same material at the same trade rate simply travels a shorter road to your home.

About the numbers on this page

Competitor figures are indicative ranges published by Livspace and HomeLane on their own sites and cost guides in 2025 and 2026. Every home differs, so treat them as honest starting points, not final quotes.

Where the chains genuinely win

If you have a standard 2BHK, want catalogue finishes and need keys in 45 days flat, a national chain is a fair choice. The moment you want custom sizes, better materials or any civil change, the maths flips to us.

Flats vs villas

Villas cost more per sq ft everywhere, because staircases, double-height walls, outdoor areas and civil changes add work a flat never has. The gap between us and the chains widens on villas, because for them that work is an exception and for us it is routine.

Watch the scope, not the headline

Chain packages often exclude painting, civil repair, curtain tracks, appliances and loose furniture. Our number includes the full scope listed above. Compare only like-for-like line items.

Measure carpet, not super area

Builders sell super built-up area; interiors happen on carpet area, which is 25 to 35 percent smaller. Every range above is on carpet area. A quote priced on super area quietly charges you for walls and shafts.

Design-build under one roof

One accountable name

Villa timelines, in writing

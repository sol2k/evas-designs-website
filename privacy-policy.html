1. Who we are

You can reach us at +91 99991 45949 or on WhatsApp at +91 84475 12475. We do not publish a general email address; the fastest ways to contact us are phone, WhatsApp and the website contact form.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers personal information collected through evasdesigns.in, our contact form, phone calls, WhatsApp conversations, site visits, quotations, project discussions and related communication. It does not cover information collected directly by third-party platforms you choose to use — such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, Instagram, Facebook or LinkedIn — which are governed by their own privacy policies. It also does not replace a signed project agreement; where a signed agreement contains specific confidentiality or data-handling terms, those terms apply to that project.

3. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with us. Most of it is information you choose to share when enquiring about a project.

  • Contact details: your name and phone number when you submit our contact form, call us, or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Project details: project type, location, plot or floor-plan details, budget range, timeline, requirements, preferences and any message you send us.
  • Site and design material: photographs, videos, floor plans, approvals, measurements and documents you share for feasibility, design or estimation.
  • Communication records: notes from calls, WhatsApp chats, consultation details and follow-up requirements.
  • Technical information: basic device, browser and access information that may be visible in hosting or content-delivery logs when you browse the website.

Please do not share sensitive personal information, financial account passwords, OTPs, identity documents or confidential third-party material unless we specifically ask for it and you are comfortable sharing it for a legitimate project purpose.

4. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to respond to you and to evaluate, plan and deliver our services. Typical uses include:

  • Responding to enquiries and scheduling consultations or site visits.
  • Understanding your requirements and preparing quotations, proposals or design direction.
  • Planning architecture, interiors, construction, renovation or facade work for your project.
  • Coordinating with our in-house team, consultants, vendors and site professionals where required for your project.
  • Maintaining records of discussions, estimates, agreements and project decisions.
  • Improving our website, services, portfolio presentation and client communication.
  • Complying with legal, tax, accounting or regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your project photographs, name, address or testimonial in marketing without your permission, except where a separate agreement or written approval allows it.

5. Contact form, WhatsApp & phone

Our website contact form is designed to open WhatsApp with your message pre-filled; the form itself does not store your submission on this website. When the form opens WhatsApp, your message is sent through WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp’s terms and privacy practices. Phone calls and WhatsApp messages may be noted by our team so we can respond accurately and continue the conversation. Please avoid sending highly sensitive information through WhatsApp unless it is necessary and you accept the platform’s own security and privacy model.

6. Cookies, analytics & server logs

This website is a static marketing website and does not itself use advertising cookies or behavioural tracking pixels. Like most websites, our hosting and content-delivery providers may process standard server logs — such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and approximate access time — for security, performance and abuse prevention. If we add analytics, advertising or cookie-based tools in the future, we will update this policy before relying on them.

7. When we share information

We share personal information only where needed for the purposes above, and we try to keep it limited. This may include sharing project details with:

  • Our in-house architects, interior designers, engineers and project managers working on your enquiry or project.
  • Structural consultants, approval professionals, contractors, vendors or site specialists where their input is needed for your project.
  • Hosting, CDN, communication or IT service providers that support our website and business operations.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants or legal counsel where required.
  • Government or regulatory authorities where disclosure is required by law.

Some service providers — including WhatsApp and mapping or social platforms you choose to open from our site — may process information outside India. Their handling of your information is governed by their own policies.

8. Data retention

We keep enquiry and project information for as long as needed to respond to you, deliver services, maintain business records, resolve disputes and meet legal or accounting obligations. Enquiry information that does not lead to a project may be retained for a reasonable period so we can respond to follow-ups and understand demand for our services. When information is no longer needed, we aim to delete it or de-identify it, unless retention is required by law or a signed agreement.

9. How we protect information

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect personal information, including limiting access to team members who need it, using business communication channels responsibly, and avoiding unnecessary collection or sharing. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security; if we become aware of a breach that affects your information, we will take appropriate steps under applicable law.

10. Your rights & choices

Subject to applicable Indian data protection law, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or request deletion where retention is not required for legal, contractual or legitimate business purposes. You may also choose not to share optional project details, though this may limit our ability to respond accurately. To make a request, contact us by phone or WhatsApp using the details in Section 14. We may need to verify your identity before acting on certain requests.

11. Children’s privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults planning residential or commercial projects. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it where required.

12. Third-party links & services

Our website links to third-party platforms such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of those platforms. Information you share with them is governed by their own policies, not this Privacy Policy.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website tools, legal requirements or business practices. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last reviewed. Material changes will be posted on this page. Continued use of the website or continued communication with us after an update means the updated policy applies going forward.

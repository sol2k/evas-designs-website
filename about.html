A studio that draws and builds with the same hands.

Evas Designs is a design-build studio based in DLF Phase 3, Gurugram — practising across Delhi NCR for over a decade, with design and execution kept deliberately under one roof.

Our story

Ten years of turning drawings into addresses.

We started Evas Designs because we kept watching good design die in the handover between a designer and a contractor. So we became both. Today our architects, interior designers, site engineers and project managers sit in the same studio in Gurugram — and answer to the same standard.

Over 180 projects later — residences, villas, offices and commercial spaces across Gurugram, Delhi, Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad — the model hasn't changed: one team, one contract, one accountable partner from first sketch to final handover.

What we believe

Principles we build by

The same four rules, on every site, whether it's a bathroom refresh or a ground-up villa.

One point of accountability

Design and construction under one contract. When something needs a decision, there's exactly one phone number to call — ours.

Design that survives the site

We detail for how things are actually built in NCR — local labour, local materials, local climate — so the 3D render is what you move into.

Transparent budgets

Itemised estimates before work begins, weekly reporting during it. No vague lump sums, no surprise escalations at handover.

Craft over decoration

Junctions, reveals, light temperature, hardware — the details you touch every day get the most attention, not the least.

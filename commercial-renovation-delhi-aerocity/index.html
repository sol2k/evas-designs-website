Worked example

A 4,000 sq ft office in Saket at standard renovation grade, Rs 1,750 per sq ft, lands near Rs 70 lakh all-in including deep cleaning and night working premiums. The same organisation relocating would spend several times that in deposits, fresh fit-out and downtime.

What moves the number

Glass cabin ratio, AV load, access difficulty, after-hours share of the work and building age move quotes by up to thirty percent. Two offices of identical size can legitimately quote forty lakh apart; the audit is in the line items, which we provide open-book.

The renovation difference

Renovate, relocate or rebuild: the arithmetic for Delhi offices.

Delhi's tighter vacancy market makes this decision sharper than in Gurgaon: good floors are scarce, and the deposit plus fit-out cost of a move is punishing. The honest arithmetic favours renovation whenever the building's bones and lease allow it.

  • Relocate: deposits plus new fit-out, Rs 3,200 to 4,800 per sq ft equivalent, plus months of disruption
  • Rebuild floor plate: only when structure and lease justify it; we say so when they do not

04 · The Process How a Delhi commercial renovation runs

Four stages, built around the building's rules.

Delhi towers have their own access windows, material movement rules and approval chains. Our schedules are written around them before mobilisation, which is why our Delhi commercial projects finish on the dates we quote.

01

Survey and scope, 3 to 5 days

Site measurement, services audit, building access and material-window review, and an operations interview with your admin team. You receive a line-item scope with a fixed price and a written timeline that respects the building's rules.

02

Approvals and mobilisation, 1 to 2 weeks

Landlord and facility management sign-offs, municipal and fire coordination where the change is substantial, material lock-in at contract rates, and crew scheduling around your working calendar including night windows.

03

Phased execution, 6 to 14 weeks

Zoned demolition, then parallel civil, MEP, ceiling and joinery trades with weekly photo reports and a fixed review call. Noisy trades stay in after-hours slots; your team keeps working on the rest of the floor.

04

Deep clean and handover, 3 to 5 days

Professional deep cleaning, marble crystallisation, air purge, snag rectification and a joint walkthrough against the original scope. Handover means your staff can sit and work the same morning, not after a week of cleaning.

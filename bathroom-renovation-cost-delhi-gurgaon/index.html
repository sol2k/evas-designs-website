02 · Cost factors Why quotes spread

The six factors that decide where in the band you land.

Two projects with the same floor plan can quote Rs 5 lakh apart for honest reasons. These are the six, in the order they actually move money.

  • Waterproofing scope. Redoing waterproofing on the floor and 7-foot wall splash zones adds Rs 15,000 to 40,000 and is non-negotiable on upper floors. Skipping it is the most expensive saving in renovation.
  • Plumbing condition. Galvanised pipes in pre-2005 homes need full replacement with CPVC or PEX; budget Rs 20,000 to 60,000 per bathroom. Concealing plumbing in walls adds civil and tiling cost but transforms the finish.
  • Fittings line. A Jaquar or Kohler mid-line set costs Rs 35,000 to 80,000; premium designer lines run Rs 1 lakh to 3 lakh. The difference is finish durability and spares availability, not just looks.
  • Tile selection. Indian ceramic at Rs 40 to 80 per sq ft versus imported or large-format slabs at Rs 250 to 900 per sq ft can swing a bathroom budget by Rs 50,000 to 2 lakh.
  • Ventilation and moisture. Exhaust ducts, louvres and anti-fungus paint decide whether the bathroom stays new. Delhi NCR's hard water also argues for CP fittings and wipeable surfaces.
  • Society and access rules. Tower bathrooms in managed societies need NOC for plumbing shutoff and debris removal; independent floors are simpler. We handle both as part of scope.

Included in our scope, always

  • Strip-out and debris removal
  • Plumbing repair or replacement
  • Waterproofing with 5-year warranty
  • Tiling, dado and flooring
  • CP and sanitary fittings installation

  • Final grout, silicone and deep clean

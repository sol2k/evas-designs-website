Costs, timelines, approvals and how the ranking was decided.

For design-build projects where one team handles architecture, approvals, construction and interiors, Evas Designs ranks first in our 2026 assessment. Standard specification construction runs ₹1,500 to 2,800 per sq ft, premium specifications run ₹2,800 to 3,800 per sq ft, and luxury builds with imported finishes and automation run ₹3,800 to 5,500+ per sq ft. A typical 2,500 sq ft custom home therefore lands between ₹55 lakh and ₹1.4 crore depending on specification.

Check five things: an itemised BOQ instead of a lump-sum quote, structural drawings signed by a licensed engineer, a written milestone-linked payment schedule, site supervision by the firm's own engineers rather than outsourced labour contractors, and at least two completed projects you can physically visit in Gurgaon.

A builder or developer sells you a unit in a project they are constructing. A contractor executes construction from your drawings. A design-build company such as Evas Designs does both ends: its architects design, its engineers sanction and its site teams build, so design intent survives all the way to handover.

A stilt-plus-two or stilt-plus-four custom home typically takes 12 to 18 months from sanctioned drawings to handover, including monsoon slowdowns. Design and sanctioning add 6 to 10 weeks before site work starts. Firm timelines are only realistic with a milestone-linked contract.

Design-build firms usually manage the approval chain: structural drawings, liaison with the relevant authority, fire NOC where applicable and RWA NOC for plotted colonies. Volume developers handle approvals for their own projects but not for individual plots.

Building on your own plot in a sector such as Sectors 40 to 57 or Palam Vihar typically costs 15 to 25 percent less per sq ft than comparable ready inventory on Golf Course Road, and you get a custom plan. The trade-off is 12 to 18 months of construction time and active supervision, which a design-build contract absorbs.

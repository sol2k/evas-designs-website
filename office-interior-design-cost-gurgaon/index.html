1 Written itemised scope, no surprise bills

Quick answer: Office interior fit-out in Gurgaon in 2026 costs Rs 800 to 1,200 per sq ft basic, Rs 1,400 to 2,500 mid-range and Rs 2,500 to 5,000 or more premium, excluding GST (18 percent, usually creditable for GST-registered businesses). Worked example, 5,000 sq ft warm shell

Mid-spec at Rs 1,400 to 2,500 per sq ft: Rs 70 lakh to 1.25 crore for the fit-out. At a typical 80 to 100 sq ft per seat that is 50 to 60 workstations, putting the all-in cost per seat around Rs 1.2 to 2 lakh including the furniture and IT-AV scope. Premium boardrooms and a feature reception push the top of the band.

The GST note finance teams miss

Fit-out works attract 18 percent GST, but a GST-registered business can usually claim it as input tax credit against its output liability, making the tax effectively cost-neutral. Structure the invoice correctly from day one; retrofitting the credit is painful.

Where quotes hide the spread

Three places: whether furniture is in the rate, whether HVAC is equipment-included or ducting-only, and whether the rate is on carpet or leasable area. A Rs 1,200 quote on carpet with furniture can cost more than a Rs 1,500 quote on leasable area without it. We quote one basis, itemised, always.

02 · Shell types The lease decides the budget

Warm shell versus cold shell, the 40 percent variable.

What the landlord hands over decides what you must build. Confirm the shell condition in the lease before budgeting; it is a negotiation point, not a footnote.

HandoverLandlord providesYour fit-out addsBudget impact
Cold shellBare structure, floor slab, plumbing risers onlyEverything: HVAC, electrical distribution, fire detection, plumbing, toiletsRs 2,500 to 5,000+ per sq ft
Warm shellBasic MEP: electrical panels, HVAC risers, fire sprinkler mainsPartitions, flooring, ceiling, lighting, furniture, distribution from risersRs 1,500 to 3,000 per sq ft
Fully fittedMove-in-ready space with basic furnitureBranding, IT setup, furniture upgrades onlyRs 200 to 800 per sq ft

CAT A versus CAT B, in plain words

CAT A is the landlord's base-building standard: lobbies, lifts, core services. CAT B is your tenant fit-out: the design, the partitions, the furniture, the brand. Most Gurgaon Grade A towers hand over CAT A warm shells and the tenant funds CAT B. Knowing which side of the line every rupee sits on is the whole budgeting game.

Negotiate the shell before the rent

A landlord who upgrades the shell, extends the rent-free period or contributes to fit-out is cutting your real cost more than a small rent discount. We review lease technical annexes for clients before signing, because the shell clause is worth lakhs either way.

  • Timeline reality. 12 to 20 weeks from design start to move-in for a 3,000 to 10,000 sq ft office, with fire NOC and building permissions adding a 2 to 3 week buffer when they apply.
  • Server room exception. A proper server room (raised floor, dedicated cooling, suppression) prices separately at Rs 3,000 to 8,000 per sq ft for its own area; it never fits inside the per-sq-ft average.
  • Per-seat planning. Budget 80 to 100 sq ft of leased area per seat for open plans with meeting rooms; dense benching goes lower, cabin-heavy law and consulting floors go higher.
  • Acoustics are not optional. Open plans without acoustic ceilings and panels cost you productivity complaints within a quarter; treatment is 8 to 12 percent of fit-out and pays back immediately.
  • Hybrid-era AV. Every meeting room is now a video room; camera, mic and acoustic treatment per room run Rs 2 to 8 lakh depending on tier, and retrofitting costs double.

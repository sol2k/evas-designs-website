01 — Included What we handle

House Construction from Scratch in Gurgaon: scope that converts intent into action.

A smooth construction journey starts before excavation. We align the design, budget, materials, consultants and site sequence so the project has a workable plan from day one.

Plot assessment

We review access, levels, orientation, soil-related consultant inputs where needed, nearby construction and the practical realities that affect design and cost.

Home design & planning

Architectural layouts, elevations and interior coordination are resolved early so the construction team is not forced to improvise on site.

Budget planning

A realistic cost framework is built around structure, finishes, services, approvals and the level of specification you want for the home.

Construction-stage planning

The work is sequenced from excavation and structure to masonry, services, plaster, flooring, joinery, painting and handover.

Material selection

We help shortlist structural finishes, flooring, stone, sanitaryware, doors, lighting and exterior materials based on durability, budget and maintenance.

Site supervision & reporting

Where included, our team coordinates site progress, quality checks and vendor communication with regular updates for you.

04 — Questions Before you enquire

Common questions about house construction.

Answers are intentionally specific so you can decide whether a consultation is worth your time.

We support the journey from plot assessment and architectural design through construction planning and coordination. The exact execution model is confirmed after reviewing your plot, scope and preferred level of involvement.

Turnkey scope can be discussed where the project requirements, timelines and responsibilities are clearly defined. We avoid using the term loosely; your proposal will state exactly what is included.

Budget planning considers structure, built-up area, specifications, services, approvals, material choices and finish level. Early estimates are ranges; a detailed budget becomes reliable after drawings and specifications are developed.

Site supervision can be included based on the agreed scope. Where included, the project receives coordinated site checks, vendor communication and progress reporting.

Ideally before purchasing materials, hiring separate vendors or finalising a floor plan. Early planning prevents rework, structural compromises and budget drift later.

03 — What happens at a studio visit Worth the trip

Design decisions are easier when you can touch the materials.

A studio visit is where ideas become decisions — most clients leave with a clearer scope than they arrived with.

01

Walk through your brief

Bring a floor plan, photos or just a Pinterest board — we map your requirements to a realistic scope and budget range.

02

Review materials & finishes

See and feel stone, veneer, laminates, fabrics and hardware instead of choosing from phone screenshots.

03

See design work in 3D

For projects already in design, review 3D elevations and walkthroughs on a proper screen with the designer who made them.

04

Leave with a plan

Next steps, an itemised estimate approach and a timeline — in writing, with no obligation to proceed.

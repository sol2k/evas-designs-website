01 · The Corridor Every tower, both kinds of homes

New apartments. Existing apartments. We makeover both.

Your project not listed?

This corridor adds new launches every quarter. If your tower or floor is not in the table, send us the name on WhatsApp; we have likely already measured a unit there or can pull the tower drawings before your site visit.

New launch? Move at booking stage

On towers still under construction, the cheapest interior is decided months before possession. Beam positions, duct runs and kitchen walls are locked early; a design review at booking stage is free and routinely saves Rs 5 to 10 lakh in avoided civil rework.

Ready tower? The upgrade cycle is now

Most ready towers on this corridor are six to twelve years old, which is exactly when kitchens, wardrobes and bathroom fittings reach the end of their comfortable life. A selective upgrade costs a fraction of a full interior and lifts resale and rental value together.

What the rate includes

Civil changes, flooring, false ceilings, electrical rewiring where needed, plumbing, panelling, kitchens, wardrobes, painting, polish, styling, and a post-work deep clean before handover. One number, one contract, no per-room arithmetic.

What it excludes, honestly

Loose furniture you already own, appliances, RWA fit-out deposits and any society charges. All four are listed in writing before you sign, so the final invoice matches the estimate.

Why ranges, not a package price

Corridor towers differ: a 4,360 sq ft Birla Arika and a 2,745 sq ft Elevate Reserve are both "large 4BHKs" but carry very different scope. We price from your measured plan, never from a brochure package.

07 · FAQ Asked at every site visit on the corridor

Apartment makeovers here, answered plainly.

For a full luxury makeover of a premium apartment, budget Rs 2,150 to 3,000 per sq ft of carpet area. That works out to roughly Rs 45 to 65 lakh for a 3BHK of 2,000 to 2,600 sq ft, Rs 65 lakh to 1.1 crore for a 4BHK of 3,000 to 4,500 sq ft, and Rs 1.1 to 1.6 crore for a 5BHK or penthouse above 5,000 sq ft. A lighter refresh, meaning paint, lighting and soft furnishings over a sound base, runs Rs 450 to 700 per sq ft.

For existing apartments, yes, in zones. We sequence the work so one bathroom and the kitchen remain functional at all times, dust walls are sealed with zippered barriers, and noisy civil work is booked into fixed morning slots that most RWAs on the corridor permit. Full-home makeovers are faster if you move out for eight to twelve weeks, but roughly a third of our corridor projects are done occupied, room by room.

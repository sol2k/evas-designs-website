For full-home interiors, Delhi studios typically charge a design fee of Rs 60 to Rs 250 per sq ft of carpet area, or fold design into a design-and-execute package at Rs 1,200 to Rs 4,200 per sq ft all-in depending on specification. Premium South Delhi work sits at the top of the range; East and West Delhi at the bottom. The honest comparison is the itemised scope behind the number: wardrobes, kitchen specification, ceiling and lighting scope, and site supervision.

A 2BHK of roughly 900 to 1,100 sq ft carpet area typically runs Rs 14 to 25 lakh for a well-finished full interior in 2026: modular kitchen, wardrobes, false ceilings, flooring, electrical and painting. Essential packages start around Rs 10 lakh; premium specifications with custom joinery and imported finishes go higher. Materials drive the range, which is why we freeze selections at design stage.

01

Interior Design

Complete interior design for homes, villas and offices — from spatial planning and lighting design to material palettes, custom furniture and styling. We design for how a space is lived in: circulation, storage, acoustics and light come before ornament.

Space planning 3D visualisation Lighting design Custom furniture Material & finish boards
02

Architecture & Building Design

Concept design through sanction-ready working drawings for residences, villas and commercial buildings. We design around plot orientation, natural light, ventilation and local bye-laws — so the building performs as beautifully as it photographs.

Concept & schematic design Working drawings Sanction drawings Structural coordination
03

Full Construction

Ground-up construction of homes, offices and commercial spaces, executed by our own site teams with daily supervision. Structure, masonry, services, finishes — every trade sequenced and quality-checked by us, with weekly progress reports to you.

Turnkey construction Site supervision MEP services Quality audits
04

Renovation & Remodeling

Reworking an existing structure's layout, systems and finishes without starting from zero. We specialise in occupied-home and live-office renovations — phased so your life or business keeps running around the work.

Layout reconfiguration Kitchen & bathroom remodels Electrical & plumbing upgrades Phased execution
05

Structural Rebuilds

Where a building's bones need to change — floor additions, load-bearing modifications, staircase relocation, structural strengthening — engineered, approved and executed to code, with structural consultants on our team.

Floor additions Load-bearing alterations Structural audits Strengthening & retrofit
06

Facade & Beautification

Refreshing what a building shows the street — facade redesign, entrance experiences, boundary walls, signage zones and exterior lighting. The fastest way to transform a property's presence and value without touching its plan.

Facade redesign Entrance & lobby upgrades Exterior lighting Cladding & finishes

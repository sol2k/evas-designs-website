  • Lighting design — layered ambient, task and accent schemes, automation-ready
  • Material & finish boards — physical palettes you approve before anything is ordered
  • Custom furniture & styling — designed pieces, not catalogue compromises

03 — The process How every project runs

From first sketch to final handover.

The same four-step spine runs under every service — whether it's a 3D package or a full build.

01

Listen & inspect

A free site visit and a long conversation about how you live or work. We measure, photograph and assess structure, services and compliance obligations before proposing anything.

02

Design & visualise

Concepts, floor plans and photorealistic 3D elevations and interior renders. You approve what the home will actually look like — on screen, not in your imagination.

03

Price & contract

An itemised, line-by-line BOQ with quantities and rates. One contract, a milestone payment schedule, and nothing signed until the numbers make sense to you.

04

Build & hand over

Our in-house teams execute with a dedicated project manager, weekly photo-and-budget reports, stage-wise quality checks and a documented handover with warranties.

Where the money goes

The line items that decide your final number.

Across our Delhi NCR renovation projects, roughly 30 to 40 percent of the budget goes to civil and structural work, 25 to 35 percent to kitchens, bathrooms and joinery, 15 to 20 percent to electrical, plumbing and waterproofing, and the rest to finishes, paint and contingencies. Older homes almost always need more services work than owners expect, which is why we survey wiring and plumbing before quoting, not after.

02 · Compare Renovate, remodel or rebuild

Three options, priced honestly.

Most Delhi NCR properties worth improving deserve all three priced in writing. Here is how the decision usually runs.

01

Renovate: refresh what exists

Best when the layout works and the structure is sound. Paint, floors, kitchens, bathrooms and services at Rs 800 to Rs 3,000 per sq ft, with you back in the house in weeks, not months.

02

Remodel: change the layout

Best when the house works but the plan does not. Walls move, rooms merge, services reroute, at Rs 1,800 to Rs 3,000 per sq ft for premium scope. The middle path most South Delhi and Gurgaon owners take.

03

Rebuild: start from the plinth

Best when the structure is spent. Full construction at Rs 1,500 to Rs 5,500 per sq ft, 10 to 16 months, and a house that behaves like new because it is. We will tell you honestly when this is the better spend.

04

Get all three in writing

We price the same property as renovation, remodelling and rebuild as separate written options, so the decision is made on real numbers rather than guesswork or a contractor's preference.

A realistic 2026 working range for a full-house renovation in Delhi NCR is Rs 800 to Rs 1,800 per sq ft for a standard refresh, Rs 1,800 to Rs 3,000 per sq ft for a premium renovation with layout changes and new services, and Rs 3,000 to Rs 5,000 plus per sq ft for a luxury gut renovation. A typical 2,000 sq ft home therefore lands between roughly Rs 16 lakh and Rs 60 lakh depending on scope. The honest way to compare quotes is an itemised scope, not a per-sq-ft headline.

Renovation refreshes finishes: paint, flooring, kitchens, bathrooms, electrical and plumbing updates, with the structure untouched. Remodelling changes the layout: walls move, rooms merge or split, services get rerouted. Rebuilding replaces the structure, usually keeping only the plinth. Costs climb sharply across the three, so we always price a Delhi NCR property as separate written options where the structure allows it.

Three reasons. First, scope: one quote includes kitchens, bathrooms, services and false ceilings, another only covers paint and flooring. Second, existing condition: a 30-year-old house often hides plumbing, wiring and waterproofing work that a newer one does not. Third, specification: the same room can be finished at Rs 1,500 or Rs 4,500 per sq ft depending on materials. Only itemised quotes can be compared line by line.

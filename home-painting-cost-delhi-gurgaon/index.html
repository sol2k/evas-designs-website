The paintable-area rule

Paintable area equals walls plus ceilings minus doors and windows, and for a standard flat it runs 3 to 3.5 times the carpet area. A contractor quoting Rs 30 per sq ft on carpet area is quoting Rs 105 per sq ft on paintable area. Always ask which area the rate is on, in writing.

What two coats really means

Full preparation is one coat of primer plus two coats of emulsion over repaired putty. Quotes that skip putty or primer look 15 to 25 percent cheaper and deliver walls that show every patch within a year. Our rates never separate preparation from paint.

03 · Honest quoting Where painting quotes hide margin

Five places a cheap painting quote gets its money back.

Painting is a commodity job quoted like a mystery. After 180+ projects, these are the five margin-hiding moves we see most, and the written clauses that neutralise them.

  • Area basis. Carpet-area rates look 60 to 70 percent cheaper than wall-area rates for identical work. Our quotes state paintable area measured room by room, with deductions for openings over four sq ft.
  • Putty coat count. Fresh putty everywhere costs Rs 4 to 6 per sq ft; honest repaints touch up only damaged patches. Quotes that promise full putty at economy prices either dilute it or skip it.
  • Single-coat primer skipping. Primer is invisible in the final wall, so it is the first thing cut. Walls without primer fade unevenly within two summers, especially on Gurgaon's sun-facing west walls.
  • Dilution ratios. Economical thinning stretches paint at the cost of coverage and washability. We specify brand-can ratios in the quote: one litre per 140 to 160 sq ft per coat for premium emulsions.
  • Colour-count games. Every extra colour beyond three adds masking, edging and small-pot waste. We price colours four and above per room honestly instead of recovering it inside a vague lump sum.

  • Patch putty, crack filling and one primer coat
  • Two coats of the named paint at brand-can ratio
  • Ceiling painting included, same rates

When should you paint in Delhi NCR?

October to March is ideal: dry air, fast curing, and pre-Diwali convenience. Avoid peak monsoon weeks, when humidity above 75 percent extends drying and risks blushing. Exterior work also faces winter GRAP restrictions in Delhi, so plan facade painting for spring or autumn. Read our GRAP construction-ban guide for the seasonal rules that affect exterior work.

02 · Cost factors Why quotes spread

The six factors that decide where in the band you land.

Two projects with the same floor plan can quote Rs 5 lakh apart for honest reasons. These are the six, in the order they actually move money.

  • Carpet area honesty. The same home prices 15 to 25 percent lower per sq ft on built-up area than carpet. Every number on this page is carpet area; demand the same from any vendor.
  • South versus East Delhi. South Delhi homes run 10 to 20 percent higher on identical scope: older stock needs more prep, clients choose higher-grade finishes, and DDA versus private-belt logistics differ.
  • Age of the property. A 20-year-old South Delhi floor needs electrical, plumbing and flooring prep before joinery; a new tower needs almost none. Survey-first quoting exists because of this line item alone.
  • Ceiling and lighting coverage. Full-home ceilings with layered lighting add Rs 100 to 220 per sq ft to any tier; selective ceilings save 40 percent of that with most of the visual return.
  • Joinery quantity. Wardrobes, TV units, study and mandir joinery is where budgets actually move: each full-height built unit runs Rs 45,000 to 1.5 lakh depending on tier.
  • Service relocation. Moving the kitchen wet wall or adding points in an occupied Delhi home means civil patchwork; in bare shells it is trivial. Occupied-home interiors carry a 5 to 10 percent logistics premium.

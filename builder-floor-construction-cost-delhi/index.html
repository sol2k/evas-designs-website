02 · Cost factors Why quotes spread

The six factors that decide where in the band you land.

Two projects with the same floor plan can quote Rs 5 lakh apart for honest reasons. These are the six, in the order they actually move money.

  • Floor count and FAR. Delhi's bylaws give more built area across more floors on wider roads; a 40-foot road plot builds differently from a 30-foot internal lane plot, and the FAR decision is worth lakhs before design starts.
  • Lift decision. A lift costs Rs 6 to 10 lakh but changes who buys: families with parents pay a clear premium for it, and third-floor rent without a lift is structurally lower.
  • Facade and frontage. On a 30-foot frontage the facade is the entire marketing budget: Rs 3 to 8 lakh of cladding and glazing routinely returns Rs 15 to 25 lakh in sale premium.
  • Parking count. Stilt can hold two to four cars depending on plot width and ramp design; one parking space in South Delhi is worth real money, so the stilt plan is a financial decision.
  • MCD sanction and completion. Sanction drawings, completion certificate and individual floor regularisation drive the calendar; builders who start without complete papers hand you a beautiful, unsellable floor.
  • Terrace rights and top floor. The top floor with terrace rights commands the highest price; waterproofing and access design there decide whether that premium survives the first monsoon.

Included in our scope, always

  • Stilt parking plan with ramp and ramp-cum-stair option

Full-home interior design in Gurgaon and Delhi NCR runs Rs 950 to 1,400 per sq ft of carpet area at standard specification, Rs 1,600 to 2,200 premium and Rs 2,400 to 3,500 or more for luxury finishes, plus 18 percent GST. A 3BHK typically budgets Rs 18 to 35 lakh for complete interiors. Every Evas Designs quote is an itemised BOQ with named brands, so the estimate you approve is the invoice you pay.

Yes, that is the entire point of the studio. Evas Designs is a design-build firm: in-house architects, interior designers and site engineers carry one scope from concept and sanction drawings through construction and interiors to handover. One contract, one itemised BOQ, one team responsible for the result, and no translation loss between a designer and a separate contractor.

We work across Delhi NCR: Gurugram, Delhi, Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad. Our studios are at W5/3, DLF Phase 3, Gurugram and C-71, Shivalik Colony, Malviya Nagar, New Delhi, open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00. Most of our 180+ projects are in Gurgaon and South Delhi, and we take select outstation projects on request.

With separate firms you manage the interface: the architect blames the contractor, the contractor blames the drawings, and you pay for the gaps. In design-build, the same team that draws also builds, so details are resolved before they reach the site, budgets are priced by the people who will execute them, and there is exactly one party accountable for quality, cost and timeline.

Both. Residential work spans apartments, builder floors, villas and farmhouses; commercial work covers offices, retail showrooms, restaurants and cafes across Gurgaon and Delhi NCR. Commercial fit-outs run on the same in-house model with phased execution for running businesses.

Full-home interiors for a 3BHK run 3 to 6 months from design freeze to handover. Ground-up house construction runs 12 to 18 months depending on floors and specification. Renovations run 2 to 8 months by depth. We publish stage-wise schedules before work starts and report progress weekly with photographs.

Call or WhatsApp us, or send the contact form, and we schedule a site visit. After the visit you receive a written scope and an itemised estimate, free and with no obligation. Design begins only once you approve the scope, and execution begins only once you approve the design.

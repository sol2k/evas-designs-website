02 — Process How we work

How a project moves with us

Four stages, the same team throughout. You always know who to call and what happens next.

01

Site visit & brief

We walk the site, understand how you'll use the space, and set a realistic scope and budget together.

02

Design & planning

Layouts, material boards and 3D visuals, refined with you until the plan is one you're confident in.

03

Execution

Handover

A final walkthrough, snag-list closure, and documentation handed over with the keys.

What actually moves the number

Two houses of the same size in the same colony can differ by lakhs. The drivers, in order of impact:

  • Finish quality — flooring, bathrooms and kitchens move the rate most
  • Design complexity — cantilevers, curves and double heights cost more than simple spans
  • Floors & structure — each additional floor adds steel, RCC and staircase work (~4%)
  • Site conditions — soil, water table and plot access affect foundation cost
  • Extras — basements, lifts, solar and home automation are priced separately

Heat costs money too: west-facing, glass-heavy buildings in NCR pay for it every summer. See why buildings get hot in summer — the cooling design decisions worth making before you build.
